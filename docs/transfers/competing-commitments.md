# Competing fulfilment and reversal commands

The actor, source document, child-row identity, operating company and already committed quantity determine whether a follow-up command can proceed. A draft prepared yesterday can remain internally balanced after another transaction has consumed its entitlement. The examples below identify what to reload, which records change, and which records retain their balances.

## Reserve, fulfil and release the last units

For stock held without individual batch or serial allocation, read available stock for the item and warehouse. Deduct remaining submitted reservations as reserved less delivered, transferred and consumed, omitting the current reservation when that operation requires it. The aggregate reservation lookup requests a lock. A batch follows its batch-specific availability path. Transferring a reservation from a predecessor also looks up entries by predecessor type, document, exact line and item, oldest first, with a lock.

Two sales order rows each request four units from an opening stock of six. Admitting the first reservation of four leaves two available to the other order. A request to reserve all four remaining on the second order exceeds availability. No delivery or invoice has yet arisen. Once three units of the first reservation are delivered, actual stock is three and its outstanding reservation is one. Two are still available to the second order. Releasing the last reserved unit makes three available. It produces no stock receipt or customer credit. When reversing an actual delivery, trace the movement, its reservation attribution and any stock accounting legs to that delivery's row. A second caller reading the same opening six must recheck admission before accepting a reservation. The presence of a lock in one lookup alone cannot establish the outcome for all transaction isolation levels or an empty reservation set. [Reservation attribution](../operations/identity-and-reservation.md)

## Claim a delivery for one shipment

A shipment row must identify a submitted delivery, and a customer shipment must match its delivery customer. Reject a repeated delivery identifier within the same proposed shipment. At submission, lock the delivery row and check, with a locking lookup, for another submitted shipment that already claims it. A draft does not hold the submitted claim. A linked delivery's base grand total supplies the goods value for that row.

Two clerks can each prepare a draft for delivery D, worth 120. The first accepted submission claims D. The second submission must re-read the claim and refuse D. If the accepted shipment is cancelled, it ceases to be a submitted claimant and a new eligible shipment can claim D. D stays submitted. Inventory, the original delivery's posted cost, and the customer's issued invoice are unchanged by replacing the consignment. A provider's cancel-queued response is a separate state from cancellation confirmation or credit. [Parcel and provider outcome](../operations/traceable-stock-and-service.md#quote-book-and-cancel-a-consignment)

## Close and reopen an order after partial execution

For the bulk sales-order action, check write access to the type and submission permission on each named order. Only submitted orders are considered. The close path selects an unfinished, nonclosed order and checks its stored modification time. Active explicit stock reservations block closing. The operator must unreserve them deliberately before resubmitting the close. Reopening revalidates any blanket-order commitment. For a submitted sales order reset through the ordinary reopen path, run the credit check after clearing the closed status. Refresh bin commitments, linked subcontracting status, notifications and blanket-order consumption.

Purchase order closure refreshes requested quantity, ordered quantity and subcontract reservation totals. A close or reopen also refreshes its downstream subcontract order status. Suppose a supplier delivers six of ten ordered units and the remaining four are outstanding. At 12 per unit the received inventory is 72. Closing the order withdraws the remaining four from commitments. The stock receipt stays six, its account effect stays 72 and physical receipt progress stays 60%. Reopening restores eligibility for the remaining four after its own checks. It cannot create a second receipt. If six were already billed, supplier debt remains until it is settled or credited.

An installation offering independent row closure must additionally check the present row flag when an old draft is submitted and preserve the hidden row when a form posts only its visible siblings. The primary implementation uses whole-document close and all-row percentage calculations. Apply the optional close/reopen rules only to deployments exposing those independent row actions. [Progress and close rules](../operations/transaction-progress-and-row-closure.md)

## Trace a production return through stock and service billing

For an outsourced finished item, identify the originating subcontract receipt row, its accepted and rejected quantities and their respective warehouses, then correlate consumed materials to that same originating row. A manufacturer can receive finished goods without immediately posting the supplier's invoice. A supplier credit cannot restore a serial or a batch by itself.

Consider four units accepted into stock, two rejected into inspection stock, and one supplier service per accepted unit. Returning one accepted unit reduces its accepted stock and the residual accepted return entitlement. Returning a rejected unit must select the rejected warehouse and follow the rejected-material treatment. Two return drafts referencing the same final accepted unit cannot both consume it. Re-read cumulative submitted returns by source row. A cancelled return relinquishes its prior claim against that receipt. Keep the chosen accepted/rejected source, service quantity and any submitted purchase invoice allocation visible across cancellation. A mapper creating a draft service purchase receipt has no supplier payment effect.
