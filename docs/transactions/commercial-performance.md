# Commission, target variance, installment coverage and country defaults

## Recognize and allocate commercial credit

Sales-partner commission and salesperson incentives are distinct projections. For the partner, round the selected rate at field precision and validate 0–100 inclusive. No partner gives zero rate. A missing rate, or a change of partner while leaving the rate unchanged, reads the new partner's default. Eligible amount is the sum of **company-currency net amounts of lines marked to grant commission**. Calculate partner commission = eligible amount × partner rate / 100, rounded at total-commission precision. A submitted order, invoice, delivery and counter ticket may each carry this measure in their own transaction population. Posting an expense or paying a partner is a separate transaction.

For the internal sales team, compute each person's allocated amount as the same eligible base × contribution percentage / 100; round at row amount precision. Compute incentives from this **rounded allocated amount** × that person's commission rate / 100, rounded at incentive precision. Each contribution is 0–100 inclusive and total contributions on a populated team must round to exactly 100 at the allocation field's precision. Duplicate people and group nodes are invalid. Reject a newly assigned disabled salesperson, while allowing an inherited disabled reference on a return, an amendment, or a sales invoice billing a delivery note with that team. For the same saved person/row retain the historical rate. New rows use the person's source rate for a linked return, otherwise the master rate. Typed changes cannot override this reconciliation. A return that carries a negative eligible base produces signed commission/incentive values.

With eligible sales of 2,500 and exempt goods worth 500, a 4% partner earns 100. Two people with 60% / 40% credit and 8% / 3% incentive rates receive 1,500 / 1,000 of credit and 120 / 30 of incentive. The partner summary presents **all** 3,000 of base net sales and the stored 100 commission. Multiplying its displayed amount by the displayed 4% would incorrectly produce 120.

## Compare actuals to targets

A target row belongs to a salesperson, territory or partner, a fiscal year, an optional item group and a named monthly distribution. Target quantity and amount cannot be negative. Two rows sharing fiscal year and item group (including the blank group) for one parent fail. Salesperson validation also requires nonzero quantity or amount. A monthly distribution's percentages must sum to 100 at two decimal places. Allocate by named calendar months spanned by each output period (one, three, six or twelve). This works across a fiscal year that starts outside January. Period target = annual target × summed period percentages / 100.

Select submitted permitted parents within the fiscal year and company filter, using order date for sales orders and posting date otherwise. Actual amount is line base net, actual quantity is stock quantity. For salesperson actuals multiply each line by that person's document team percentage. For partner/territory use the whole qualifying line. A territory or salesperson group rolls up its nested descendants; partners use their own identity. Item-group targets qualify actuals for the selected group and its represented descendants; a blank group aggregates all. Sum within the inclusive period. Variance = achieved − target. For a 20,000 annual target with a 20% quarter allocation, the target is 4,000. Sales of 6,000 allocated 60% give achieved 3,600 and variance **−400**. A negative return row reduces achieved results. Parent and child target rows can both include the same sales; summing the presentation tree as a flat list double-counts overlapping scopes.

## Interpret the sales-order payment terms report

The report uses submitted order installment rows and a second population of linked submitted invoices. It initially marks each due date before today Overdue and other dates Unpaid. It queries eligible order statuses, company, order date range and record-permission constraints, and offers due date/customer/item filters. Sort by order, transaction date and due date.

Starting from each stored term's paid amount, consume **positive linked invoice base grand totals** against each term's remaining scheduled amount, earliest due first. When an invoice covers the balance, mark Completed and carry unused invoice value to the next term. If it covers only part, exhaust the invoice amount and mark Partly Paid. Track contributing invoice identities. Hence the report's Paid Amount can reflect **invoiced coverage** without a customer remittance. It is unsuitable as a cash receipt reconciliation control. The report groups joined invoice items by invoice parent and selects an order identity with the full invoice amount. An invoice spanning multiple orders needs a separate allocation fixture before using this report for order-level certification. After status filtering the immediate-upcoming filter takes the first term *strictly after* today per order.

## Install country and chart defaults

Country setup accepts a compact account, rate and label mapping, or a chart-specific template collection. The selected template governs the company’s calculations.

Require an existing company. No country entry means the tax setup operation returns without adding templates. Expand simple mappings into item tax templates and default sale/purchase templates. For a company copying another company's chart, resolve the original company's chart name for selection. Prefer an exact chart profile to the wildcard. For each direction prefer its dedicated templates to a wildcard, resolve tax-category records, then create sales, purchase and item templates. Preserve a company/template pair already present rather than overwriting its rates on rerun. Fill absent transaction-template row fields with On Net Total, Total category and company cost centre, plus Add on purchase rows. Explicit fields take precedence. Item tax templates use their own row structure.

Resolve an account by company and required root type (default Liability), using account name or optional account number. Otherwise locate a Tax group in that root; then try the designated group name; finally create the tax group directly under the company balance-sheet root. Resolve/create the tax leaf below it and connect the template. A regional follow-on callback can supply country-specific rules after template creation; its failure can be logged while company setup proceeds. Example: the Namibia entry supplies the title “Namibia Tax”, account name “VAT” and rate 15. With a fresh company using this seed, the compact expansion yields sale, purchase and item template kinds using this rate. This example supplies no default flag. Changing a template later and rerunning does not reset that existing template to 15.
