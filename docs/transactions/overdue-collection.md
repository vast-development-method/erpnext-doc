# Overdue collection across invoice, notice and receipt

A notice records the overdue principal as it appeared on issue, the annual interest rate, due dates, currency and exchange rate, collection fee, income account, customer and company. Child rows identify invoice and installment. Subsequent collection uses **live** balances; preserve the amounts printed on the original notice.

## Construct a valid notice

Select an accessible submitted sales invoice, excluding returns. Company, customer and transaction currency must agree with the notice. Each ordinary invoice row references one of that invoice's payment schedule rows. A submitted opening invoice with no schedule is the exception: use the invoice due date, rounded total if present, otherwise grand total, and 100 percent portion. Reject duplicate `(invoice, installment)` pairs and modified copied terms, due dates, payment method, amount or portion. The due date must precede the notice date. At field precision require a positive principal within the invoice or installment's available balance. Derive notice level as one plus the count of other submitted notice rows for that invoice and installment.

To reconstruct installment balances, express the invoice balance and schedule amounts in one currency. When the receivable account currency differs from the invoice currency the controller divides receivable-account outstanding by the invoice conversion rate to obtain invoice-currency outstanding. For an account-currency request, convert schedule amounts instead. Let `u = max(0, sum(schedule outstanding) − live invoice outstanding)`. Visit installments in ascending due date then original row order, subtracting `min(row outstanding, u)` and reducing `u` by the same amount. This allocates a receipt that reduced the invoice globally but left its individual term balances unchanged. On collection cap each historic demand by the current available balance.

Example: two installments originally remain at 500.00 each. A 400.00 payment unallocated to any installment leaves invoice outstanding 600.00. The two effective term balances are now 100.00 and 500.00. If only the first installment is overdue the new notice may demand 100.00 of principal. Refuse a demand for 500.00. The next installment becomes eligible the day **after** its due date.

## Charge and collect

Interest for each row is `principal × annual percentage ÷ 100 × calendar days overdue ÷ 365`. Use 365 even across a leap day. Sum line interest, add the notice fee, and multiply the charge total by the notice conversion rate to obtain charges in company currency. Amount payable on the notice is principal plus transaction-currency charges. A same-currency notice uses rate one. A foreign notice needs a positive rate. The configured notice-type fee is in company currency: divide by the conversion rate when defaulting the fee. When the exchange rate changes without a user override, rederive a fee that still matches the former converted default. A manually supplied fee, including an explicit zero, can survive defaulting. Require an income account to submit a notice with nonzero charges. Submission sets `Unresolved`.

Payment preparation creates one reference per live unpaid invoice installment in the receivable-account currency and a separate **negative deduction** for remaining charges in company currency. For charged interest and fees of 11.00 on principal of 100.00, full same-currency collection debits bank 111.00, credits receivables 100.00 and credits collection income 11.00. After a principal-only collection the invoice can be settled while the notice remains unresolved. Remaining charges equal base charges less the sum collected. Count only negative deduction rows on submitted customer receipts with the same notice, company, party and income account. Amounts in unrelated deductions never settle this notice. Cancelled receipts cease to qualify, allowing the outstanding to reopen.

A manually resolved notice waives the remaining charge for status refreshes that respect manual resolution and have no remaining invoiced sum. A later paid notice can supersede an earlier one only if all original rows have installment IDs, it covers **all** those IDs, shares customer, company and currency, is submitted, has charges at least as large, has no unpaid principal, and has collected its own charges independently. Eligible successors have posting date at least the earlier date and either later posting date or later creation time. Resolved-by-successor retains the successor identity. See `FIN-008`–`FIN-010` in the [vectors](examples/financial-decisions.json).
