# Trace stock, production and service decisions

## Select batches at the effective business time

The ordinary batch selector groups active stock movements by batch and warehouse, applies company, item and warehouse filters when supplied, and removes disabled batches. A dated batch remains eligible through its expiry day. An undated batch remains eligible. The expiry comparison uses today's date, even when the requested stock balance is dated earlier. The separate stock-level inquiry can include disabled and expired lots to account for physical stock still on hand. A cutoff without creation time includes movements at that posting time. With creation time supplied, only earlier-created movements at the same time qualify. Apply other reservations, point-of-sale holds and the selected ordering to the resulting supply. Earliest-expiry allocation places undated lots last. [Allocation and reservations](identity-and-reservation.md#choose-eligible-serials-and-batches)

Suppose A has four units expiring today, B has five expiring tomorrow and C has six without expiry. Two A units already belong to another reservation. An earliest-expiry request for five uses two from A and three from B. Tomorrow, an ordinary new selection excludes A even when inspecting yesterday's posting cutoff. A physical balance report may still show its two remaining units. Cancelling the delivery restores its movement and the relevant reservation attribution. It cannot extend A's shelf life.

## Return the original serial or batch

Resolve a submitted return to its original voucher **and line**. For delivery and stock-updating sales invoices a returned packed component can point to the packed row or its parent line. Follow the returned packed-row identity to the actual delivered component. Equal item codes on different components do not establish equivalence. On purchase receipts, stock-updating purchase invoices and subcontract receipts, select the accepted or rejected warehouse when it matches the source line's warehouse choices. A returned subcontract supplied-material row without a finished-line reference uses the original consumption entries. For rejected goods, exclude the separately restored consumption that follows the rejected-row rate.

| Origin and tracking | Price and admission |
|---|---|
| Original submitted bundle | Select uncancelled source entries on that item and original detail. Validate serial and batch membership during ordinary processing. With serial-specific valuation enabled, use the original serial's rate. When it is disabled, the bundle still validates identity and leaves the rate to the configured valuation path. |
| Original delivery recorded with legacy serial text or batch only | Read uncancelled, unbundled ledger rows for the original voucher, detail and item, applying the qualifying return warehouse. For an original outward movement derive the rate from the absolute value difference divided by signed quantity. An inward movement supplies its incoming rate. |
| Supplier return out of a batch valued independently | Where that warehouse's batch still has a nonzero quantity, take its current average, including a valid zero average. Without a balance, use the original receipt rate. The moving-average setting that disables batchwise valuation bypasses this override. |
| Tracked return without original valuation details | An inward return falls back to the inward pricing path. The referenced-return routine does not manufacture a matching outward price. A valuation repost has special recovery handling for unmatched recorded identities. A fresh user return must pass original-membership validation. |

A delivery issued serial S1 at 18 and S2 at 24. Its return of S2 restores one unit and 24 of carrying value. An S3 outside the original identities fails ordinary validation. For an independently valued batch, receive two at 10 and two at 20 into one warehouse. Returning one unit against the second receipt issues at 15, leaving three units worth 45. A refund at invoice price and the physical inventory reversal are distinct transactions. [Bundle decision and return sources](../rebuild/valuation/identity-and-reservation.md)

## Check dimension-specific stock before and after a movement

For a stock issue, collect every dimension that both requests negative-stock validation and has a value on the outgoing row. Sum uncancelled movement in the same company, item, warehouse and **all** those dimension values through the posting instant. Add the proposed signed quantity, round to configured float precision (default two) and reject a negative balance whose absolute value exceeds 0.0001. Future vouchers can also become insolvent. Group their movements by posting time and voucher identity and walk them in posting-time and creation order. Where a voucher defers that check, evaluate after its own rows have been applied. A stock transfer with two rows must be assessed with its complete local effects visible.

With three units at location L1 and nine at L2 in the same warehouse, issuing four from L1 fails even though the warehouse holds twelve. Issuing two today against three available today also fails if a later posted L1 issue of two would leave minus one. For a serialized outward row, compare the dimensions on the serial's last inward movement with the outward row. Treat missing and blank values alike. A serial last received at L2 cannot be issued at L1 by drawing against the other location's balance. Check custody and eligibility separately from the recorded warehouse value and tracked costing policy. [Valuation methods](rates-and-layers.md) · [Inventory counts and financial reconciliation](inventory-control.md)

For period-end value, create one item/warehouse key, a key per populated standalone inventory dimension and a combined key when multiple dimensions are populated. Batch-specific keys carry their own amounts. A bundle join can expand one ledger entry into several rows. Count the original ledger value difference only once for the item/warehouse key. At a batch or dimension key use the joined entry’s own value when available, including zero, and otherwise the ledger value. A prior closing row already belongs to exactly one key and must seed only that key. For one ledger issue of 30 split into two batch entry values of 10 and 20, warehouse cost decreases by 30 once. The two batch decreases reconcile to 30. Adding the batch subdivisions onto the warehouse loss would incorrectly show 60. [Closing value and fan-out](https://github.com/frappe/erpnext/blob/886ab6ae187244e7f1b852393384ab6019d56264/erpnext/stock/doctype/stock_closing_entry/stock_closing_entry.py#L450-L506) · [Closing key selection](https://github.com/frappe/erpnext/blob/886ab6ae187244e7f1b852393384ab6019d56264/erpnext/stock/doctype/stock_closing_entry/stock_closing_entry.py#L688-L744)

## Receive an internal transfer against the correct row

For an internal stock delivery or a sales invoice that updates stock, require the target warehouse. For an internal receipt or a purchase invoice that updates stock, require the from-warehouse. Internal transfers use the transacting company's default currency. A sales invoice containing packed items is rejected by the internal invoice path. A delivery note can expose its packed components to the receiving path through their own transferred identities.

For submission by an internal supplier, compare each non-return purchase document with the submitted source delivery or sales invoice. Key the comparison by originating detail and item. Deduct returned stock quantity using the originating row's conversion factor. Include eligible other receiving documents plus the candidate's own submitted quantities exactly once. For delivery components prefer the original packed-row reference. Add the configured over-receipt percentage to the transmitted quantity at row precision. Reject only when received exceeds that resulting limit. A cancelled receiving document ceases to contribute. Separate companies' stock and clearing records retain their identities. [Internal trading handoffs](../transactions/cross-domain-transactions.md)

A ten-unit transfer with two units returned leaves eight transferable. At a 5% allowance and two-decimal precision, the ceiling is 8.40. Five already received plus 3.40 passes this quantity check. A candidate of 3.41 fails. A second line for the same product cannot donate unused capacity to the first. Cancelling the five-unit receipt removes that contribution before computing a subsequent receipt. Authority, warehouse validity and accounting rules still apply.

## Allocate a production window

Resolve an explicit workstation or choose an enabled station of the requested type. An empty eligible type is refused. A station with no configured production capacity defaults to one. Merge overlaps from the same job card before counting how many capacity slots are occupied. Sequential intervals may share a slot when the earlier interval ends exactly as the next begins. At capacity, move the candidate after the blocking end plus the configured inter-operation gap and repeat the search. If another station of the type is available, that station may satisfy the request. Actual work logs and scheduled intervals both inform occupancy.

Unless production on holidays is enabled, shift a blocked date through consecutive holidays. Preserve the start time when moving to that next working date. Fit the remaining minutes inside operating windows. Consume the available part of one window before moving to the next. A station open 08:00–12:00 and 13:00–17:00 completes a 90-minute task starting at 11:30 at 14:00. With capacity two and two different cards busy from 09:00–10:00, a third cannot use that occupied interval on that station. Two workers' time on one card does not create two independent cards. An exact 10:00 handoff can reuse the slot when no gap applies. Replanning affects expected times. Manufacturing stock and finished quantities require their own submitted operational records. [Labor, workstation minutes and reservations](shop-floor.md)

## Quote, book and cancel a consignment

Core shipment admission checks each parcel's positive weight, count of at least one, nonnegative dimensions, pickup interval and required parties. The shipment copies the referenced submitted delivery notes' base totals. For delivery to a customer the notes must belong to that same customer. A submitted shipment requires parcel information and positive declared goods value. A core shipment cancellation changes its local status. External cancellation and any carrier credit require their own provider evidence. [Sales, parcel and booking workflow](carriage.md)

The Sendcloud adapter takes the available method's first quote and multiplies its unit parcel amount by the sum of parcel counts. A priced method with a unit quote of 7.50 and counts of two plus one therefore quotes 22.50 in the offer currency. An absent quotes list leaves the offer without a priced total. In a present quote, an omitted amount value defaults to zero in this adapter. Retain the response detail so an explicit zero can be distinguished during reconciliation. On booking the adapter stores the selected service's total price. The LetMeShip adapter presents the quoted net price, requires parcel dimensions to book, and reads the confirmed total price from the booking response. A quoted net 25 and confirmed total 30 therefore represent distinct values. They do not establish that the five-unit difference is tax. Preserve the price basis and carrier confirmation. A carrier quote alone cannot create an expense, a customer charge or a tax entry.

For Sendcloud cancellation a 200/202 response must also contain valid JSON, no provider errors and a recognized status. Queued acknowledges the request. Cancelled confirms cancellation at the provider. A transport failure, another status or malformed response leaves the outcome unresolved. Reconcile the original consignment before attempting a second booking. A provider credit, collection refund or external delivery of a notice needs separate evidence. ## Evaluate inspection readings across number formats

For each newly entered or changed, automatic numeric reading, use the operator's number format. Unrecognized formats fall back to comma for grouping and dot for decimals. A format without a decimal separator falls back to a dot and drops any conflicting grouping separator. Reject nonfinite values or malformed group sizes. When grouping appears in the integer portion, the leading group has one to three digits after an optional sign, the final group has three digits and intermediate groups have two or three. This allows familiar Indian as well as three-digit grouping. A stored unchanged string skips entry revalidation. Subsequent automatic evaluation nevertheless parses it in the evaluating operator's format.

For example, an operator using decimal comma enters `5,5` against inclusive bounds 5–6. It parses as 5.5 and passes. Under a dot-decimal/comma-grouping format the unchanged text still saves through entry validation. Numeric evaluation cannot parse that grouping and converts the missing numeric result to zero, so it fails 5–6. A replacement that stores a canonical numeric value alongside raw input can maintain a stable decision, but that is a separately identified policy. A manual reading bypasses automatic evaluation. Inclusive numeric limits require at least one populated reading and every populated reading in range. Descriptive criteria use exact text equality; a bad formula is a validation error. Cancelling an inspection used by a submitted reference document requires addressing that reference first. [Quality and maintenance decisions](quality-and-maintenance.md)

## Recompute service and calibration due dates

A maintenance log belongs to a task of the named asset maintenance plan. Completed logs require a nonfuture completion date. A date on a noncompleted log is rejected. Only completed or cancelled business outcomes can be submitted. To calculate recurrence, advance from the later applicable start or last completion using daily, weekly, calendar-month, quarterly, half-yearly or one-, two- or three-year intervals. Clear the next date when the base or last completion has reached the end date, or the candidate is beyond it. A candidate equal to the end date is allowed while its base remains earlier.

A weekly calibration completed 3 June with end date 10 June has its next due date on 10 June. Completion on 10 June produces no 17 June task. Cancelling a completed log rewinds the schedule only if that log still drives it. Its completion date must equal the task's current last completion and there must be no later-created submitted completed log for that task on the same date. If eligible, restore the greatest other submitted completion date, restore the cancelled log's due date as the next due date, and refresh the parent plan. A submitted operational log does not by itself certify an instrument's accuracy. That requires the organization's calibration evidence.

For customer maintenance, recompute an affected schedule line from its remaining submitted visits, preferring the newest fully completed visit, then the newest remaining visit. With no visit, restore Pending and clear actual date. A subsequent partial visit must not reopen a warranty claim already covered by a different submitted fully completed visit. A visit linked to a claim can be blocked from cancellation by a later submitted visit against that claim. The schedule row, claim and asset calibration task have independent identities.
