# Frappe CRM deal to ERPNext quote, customer and order

ERPNext's built-in CRM owns its own Lead, Opportunity and Prospect records. The separate Frappe CRM product owns CRM Lead, CRM Deal, CRM Organization and CRM Product. Their similar names do not make the records interchangeable. A native ERPNext opportunity drives downstream quotation status through the opportunity/quotation links. The extension deal uses explicit cross-application deal, customer and product links. Both routes ultimately hand off to the same ERP selling, stock and accounting domains. [Native opportunity workflow](../business/sales-pipeline-and-approval.md) · [CRM integration settings](https://github.com/frappe/crm/blob/9ace234fceb3c5e7bd630b74f44ba1779e831abe/crm/fcrm/doctype/erpnext_crm_settings/erpnext_crm_settings.py)

| Operation | On one site | Across sites |
|---|---|---|
| Prepare quotation | Look for ERP customer by deal link, then customer back-reference stored on deal. Quote to Customer if found. Otherwise use party kind CRM Deal and its identity. | Resolve/create a remote ERP Prospect with organization or lead name plus the CRM deal identity; prepare quote to Prospect. This step can create a remote party even before the quotation draft is saved. |
| Show linked quotations | Require enabled integration and authorized deal read. Check quotation read, then permission-filtered ERP quotes by deal identity. Return status, total, currency, issue/expiry and URL ordered by last modification. | Require readable deal. Query ERP quotations using the configured remote identity and site; remote access controls govern available results. |
| Notify deal view | An ERP quotation change with a deal reference publishes a deal-scoped refresh after commit. | A live cross-site notification is not supplied by this local hook. |
| Create customer | Target workflow stage can create on matching status. An order lacking a customer may discover the deal from its item quotation links and resolve or create its customer. An order with a customer is left as supplied. | Invoke ERP's synchronization operation. ERP must enable CRM data synchronization and permit the remote integration user when CRM is absent from that site. |
| Quote to order | The selected quote/lines carry the deal's commercial origin, become ERP order rows, and enter ordinary order validation. | ERP maps the resulting quote to its own transaction documents. Integration keys correlate the remote records. |

The quotation action returns a **prefilled new-form URL**, not a submitted quotation. For the product prefill, iterate deal product rows and resolve CRM Product's linked ERP item code. Omit rows lacking that link. Quantity uses the stored value or one, rate the stored value or zero, and discount percentage the stored value or zero. CRM rows {linked A, qty 0, rate 100, discount 10} and {unlinked B, qty 4, rate 20} therefore prepare only A with quantity 1, price list rate 100, discount 10. The ERP quotation calculates its own final value, taxes, validity and terms. No deal conversion or prefill posts stock, payable or receivable. [Quotations, lookup and prefill](https://github.com/frappe/crm/blob/9ace234fceb3c5e7bd630b74f44ba1779e831abe/crm/fcrm/doctype/erpnext_crm_settings/erpnext_crm_settings.py#L341-L549)

**Customer and contact identity.** Prefer organization name for Company. Without an organization use primary contact's full name, then deal lead name, for Individual; reject when these are missing. Carry territory, default currency, industry, website, deal identity, contacts and address. Clear territory and industry missing on the destination. The ERP customer handler finds an existing customer by **customer display name**, which can reuse a prior customer for another deal. Its allowed incoming fields constrain creation. On success write the returned ERP customer identity to the originating CRM deal. Contacts match email first, then telephone. A typed party link is appended only when the exact type/name pair is absent. A same-name organization or shared contact number therefore requires an identity reconciliation case. **Retry and visibility.** The remote prospect service reuses a prospect by company name. The customer service catches some insertion/link failures and may return no identity; address errors can be logged independently. Treat a returned customer identifier, contact links and address links as separately verifiable results. After a dropped remote reply reconcile existing parties before retrying. Returning the customer key on the deal does not demonstrate that accounting posted or that every address was synchronized. When the sales order contains lines from multiple linked quotations, the local helper returns the first deal found while scanning rows. Preserve line-level origins separately when tracking mixed deals. [Customer resolution](https://github.com/frappe/crm/blob/9ace234fceb3c5e7bd630b74f44ba1779e831abe/crm/fcrm/doctype/erpnext_crm_settings/erpnext_crm_settings.py#L616-L720)

**Catalog provenance.** ERP's item mirror can create a CRM product from item code/name and catalog data, link both identities, and suppress recursive pushes. Prefer a resolved ERP Item Price over standard rate; retain a valid zero rate. For an existing linked product update its mirrored payload only when values differ. Quotation prefill still uses the rates recorded on the deal, so updating a master is independent of renegotiating that particular deal. Product restrictions and document shares have their own mirrored lifecycles.
