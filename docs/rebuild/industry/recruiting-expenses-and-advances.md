# Recruiting commitments and employee money

## From referral to a pay event

A referral records candidate, email, vacancy designation, referring employee, resume and bonus eligibility. Reject an inactive referrer or another noncancelled referral with that email. The applicant conversion persists the source referral identity and copies candidate contact, designation and resume data. The referral then shows In Process. The applicant is a candidate. Mapping a job offer into an employee prepares a distinct employee record, including an explicit offer reference. A vacancy control on an applicable submitted staffing plan compares designation/company vacancies with the number of submitted offers dated in the planning window. With two vacancies and two submitted offers, another offer subject to the gate fails. An applicant or draft offer by itself is not a payroll party. A compensation offer can construct a prospective employee/assignment. Use joining date, else offer date, else current date, plus the offered company, structure, currency, variable and supported regional fields. Evaluate from base or solve for target annual cost to company (CTC) against that structure. An existing base already within tolerance can be reused. Report achieved CTC and whether it missed the target beyond tolerance. Display cycle gross as annual gross / periods per year for the pay frequency. The projected take-home uses gross less qualifying deductions. Employer contributions can be hidden from the displayed breakup while remaining in total cost. No offer preview posts an accrual. [Cost and offer preview](https://github.com/frappe/hrms/blob/1b2d7e034e0de8e30a9e513541d7741710587ad5/hrms/hr/doctype/job_offer/job_offer.py#L219-L393)

The bonus action prepares one additional salary linked to the referral for the **referrer**, with overwrite disabled. It checks for a prior additional salary by reference name. For a referral-based component, require a bonus-eligible Accepted referral and reject a deduction component. Submitting that additional salary sets referral payment status to Paid; cancellation restores Unpaid. That marker denotes the additional salary event. Proof of cash paid comes from payroll settlement. A repeat bonus action or a reused reference name deserves its own idempotency test. ## Request, fund, claim, settle, reverse

| Event | State and accounting |
|---|---|
| Submit advance | Positive request from an employee, configured receivable-type advance account, matching advance/account currency. An approved request establishes the limit and identity; actual funding is a separate payment. |
| Fund advance | Debit employee advance receivable, credit bank. Recalculate paid from linked non-delinked advance-ledger submit events, with sign chosen by account type. A funding total above the request is rejected. |
| Approve claim | Validate employee/company, department/company, claimant scope, row sanction ≤ amount, expense accounts, taxes and advance links. An approver cannot self-approve when prevention is enabled and no claim workflow governs the decision. A submitted claim must be Approved or Rejected. Rejection sets sanctioned amounts to zero. |
| Apply advance | Validate same employee and currency/account rules. A positive allocation requires advance account, payment reference kind and identity. Allocation cannot exceed unclaimed less returned; the sum cannot exceed sanctioned total plus applicable tax. Positive approved submitted allocations update the advance's claimed amount. |
| Post claim | Debit each expense for sanctioned value and each tax account for its tax. Credit employee advance account for allocation and employee payable for remaining grand total. Carry cost centre, project, employee and payment/advance reference through the applicable rows. |
| Settle claim | A separate payment debits employee payable, credits bank. On an immediately paid eligible claim the source can create a payable debit and bank credit inside claim posting. A claim with positive remaining grand total and linked advances clears the immediate-payment flag during validation. |
| Return advance | A cash return debits bank and credits employee advance. Salary deduction is an alternative settlement route that references the advance. A return beyond paid less claimed fails. The remaining authorized recovery must also account for already scheduled payroll deductions. |
| Cancel claim | Recompute task/project claims, cancel the claim's GL posting under its account gates, recompute reimbursement and advance-claimed amounts, and unlink claim references from payments. Already transmitted funds require their own banking event. |

In a single currency, advance 600 to employee E. Later approve an expense of 800, charge 80 tax, allocate 600. Claim grand total is **280**. The approved claim's entry is Dr expense 800, Dr tax 80, Cr advance 600, Cr E payable 280. Settle 280: Dr E payable 280, Cr bank 280. Total net bank outflow is 880 and both employee balances clear. Cancelling before reimbursement should restore 600 of advance receivable, reverse the 800 expense and 80 tax, and eliminate the 280 payable. Preserve the 600 funding voucher. Two claims against the same paid advance require a concurrency fixture, as the validation and derived totals involve different records. **Advance status precedence.** Fully claimed gives Claimed. Fully returned gives Returned. Positive claims plus positive returns together equal to paid give Partly Claimed and Returned. Otherwise a fully funded request remains Paid even if partially expensed. A request of 600, paid 600, claimed 200 and returned 0 shows Paid with 400 available; after returning the remaining 400 it becomes Partly Claimed and Returned. The displayed status alone is insufficient to compute the remaining advance.

**Foreign-currency compatibility.** Calculate each payment's historical value at its advance rate and each allocated advance's base value at the claim rate. The release accumulates line differences into a running sum, stores that running amount on each qualifying nonzero line, and adds each running sum to the total. For two row differences +10 and +20 it records +10, +30 and total **+40**; reverse their order and total becomes +50. A policy that posts only the sum of independent differences yields +30. Preserve which rule was used when reconciling imported claims.
