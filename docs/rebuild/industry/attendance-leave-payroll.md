# From a shift or approved leave to paid days

A raw time event identifies the employee, timestamp and resolved shift interval. Attendance stores a dated decision (including a half-day status), actual hours, source shift and links to events or leave. A leave ledger entry measures **days**, while a salary slip measures **pay**. Keeping all three prevents double-deducting a leave recorded both as an application and as attendance.

The [attendance and leave rules](../../people-and-services/attendance-and-leave.md) and [payroll calculation](../../people-and-services/payroll-and-benefits.md) define the inputs to this cross-domain trace.

## Complete a shift before marking a day

Require write permission for the shift. Auto attendance requires an enabled shift, a lower processing date and a synchronized check-in watermark. Select unlinked, on-shift, non-skipped logs dated on/after the lower bound with actual shift end strictly before the watermark. Equality with the watermark is still pending. Group by employee and **shift start**; an overnight shift belongs to its starting date. Calculate hours according to configured first-to-last or paired intervals, and interpret in/out according to the shift's configured identification mode. Late/early are separate flags. Equality with a grace deadline does not mark lateness or early departure.

On a half holiday halve both attendance thresholds. Compare strictly below the nonzero absent threshold first, then strictly below the nonzero half-day threshold. Otherwise mark Present. For normal thresholds of 4 and 7 hours, 4 hours is Half Day and 7 is Present. On a half holiday, 3.5 hours is Present. Updating attendance links the source check-ins. The job commits the check-in phase before the missing-day phase and commits batches in that later phase. A retry can encounter both completed and outstanding work. A shift with no synchronization evidence is insufficient to infer an absence. ## Deduct approved leave once

Submission allows Approved or Rejected; the attendance update path acts only for Approved. Process calendar days against the employee's holiday rules, materialize/adjust applicable attendance and preserve the leave-application identity. Full-day leave uses On Leave; a designated half-day uses Half Day. The leave ledger records the application and leave type, employee, allocated date boundaries, quantity and unpaid marker. Allocation changes inside an absence can require split entries and carry-forward expiry adjustments. Cancellation changes the application to Cancelled, runs the ledger cancellation path and cleans its attendance links. The cancellation query selects all noncancelled On Leave/Half Day attendance rows for the employee within the application's dates without filtering their leave-application key. This can affect overlapping records from another origin. A narrower reference-only reversal is a separate rebuild policy. A job that merely deletes a leave-request row loses the entitlement chain. For entitlement calculations see [leave entitlement](../../domains/attendance-and-leave.md). ## Derive pay from one selected policy

| Decision | Payroll outcome |
|---|---|
| Nominal working days | Start with the inclusive slip interval. With holidays excluded, remove each full holiday and half of each half holiday. Preview uses calendar days for both working and paid days. |
| Attendance vs approved-leave basis | Read payroll settings and select one source. On attendance basis, subtract eligible absence and unpaid-leave equivalents. Unmarked days can count as absent by policy. Employment start and relief dates constrain eligible pay. |
| Full partially paid leave | Unpaid equivalent = 1 − retained-pay fraction. A full day at 75% pay therefore costs **0.25 days in both paths** on an ordinary workday. |
| Half-day partially paid leave | Multiply (1 − configured half-day wage share) by (1 − retained-pay fraction). With 50% half-day wage share and 75% retained pay the result is **0.125 days**. |
| Half holiday | Full-day leave or absence on a half-working day can contribute 0.5 of a day before the retained-pay factor. The attendance half-leave branch already covers the remaining half and avoids multiplying by the half-holiday factor again. The application branch scales an excluded half holiday a second time even for a half-day leave. These branches need separate fixtures. |
| Additional component proration | For applicable structure components, the source divides payment days by **integer-converted total working days**. With total 20.5 days, pay 20, default component 2,050, the denominator in that branch is 20 and the result is 2,050. A decimal-denominator policy would yield 2,000. Additional and default parts are prorated separately and rounded at their respective precision. Timesheet and explicit overwrite branches have different rules. |

At 75% retained pay, an ordinary full day of partially paid leave costs 0.25 days under either attendance or leave-application input. With a 50% half-day wage share, an ordinary half day costs 0.125 days. On an excluded half holiday the application branch scales that half-day amount to 0.0625 days. The fixture also tests component proration with a fractional working-day denominator. See the [payroll and benefits rules](../../people-and-services/payroll-and-benefits.md).
