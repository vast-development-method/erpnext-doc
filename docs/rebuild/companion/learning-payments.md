# Learning purchases and enrolment

Learning owns the course or batch, the learner, its LMS Payment and the resulting enrolment. The payment service and shared runtime support the enrolment workflow. An admission fee charged through Education uses the separate [student fee schedule and ERP receivables path](../extensions/education-healthcare.md). A course purchase in the inspected LMS callback updates payment and access records. Posting a sales invoice or bank journal requires a separately evidenced integration. [Dependencies](https://github.com/frappe/lms/blob/0264da45e1644dc9b937620f6220d67749cb0277/pyproject.toml) · [Learning guide](https://docs.frappe.io/learning/setup-guide/your-first-steps-with-frappe-learning)

The completion service resolves a pending payment and its course or batch. Require the stored member and document type/identity to match the callback's context. Lock/check the LMS Payment to detect a retry, also check for a previously used gateway payment ID, and treat a losing unique-ID race as a duplicate. Where historic duplicate IDs prevent adding a unique constraint, keep the existing rows, index the lookup and serialize callbacks until the site can add that constraint. Resolve uncertain Razorpay availability through the recheck path. A rejected or indeterminate check cannot grant access.

For a Razorpay charge compare the reported state (authorized or captured), order receipt (the LMS payment identity), currency without case sensitivity, and amount in minor units. Prefer the nonzero stored GST-inclusive amount; otherwise use the base amount. This adapter multiplies by 100 and rejects a discrepancy of one minor unit or more. With a base of 100.00 and GST-inclusive amount of 125.00 USD, 12,500 matches; 12,499 fails. These are adapter and profile semantics, not a universal currency conversion. Once accepted, mark the LMS Payment, select certificate purchase, course enrolment or paid batch enrolment and update coupon redemption. A certificate purchase and a seat purchase produce distinct records. On course enrolment, lock the course and check whether the member already has an enrolment. A replay with the same provider reference and an existing enrolment must leave a single payment credit, a single effective member/course relation and one coupon event. Exercise this through two concurrent callbacks, verifying persisted state after their transactions complete. A live gateway comparison and ERP general-ledger reconciliation remain separate qualification gates.
