# From prospect to authorized commercial commitment

## Opportunity status follows linked quotations

Resolve the opportunity's typed party, verify customer restrictions and positive opportunity quantities, and ensure chosen contacts/addresses belong to the party. A quote can reference the opportunity on its header **or an item**. Find distinct submitted quotes linked either way. An Ordered or Partially Ordered quotation implies Converted. Otherwise, a submitted quote whose status is outside Lost, Cancelled and Expired implies Quotation. A manual edit to one of these two derived statuses must agree with that evidence; the presence of an ordered quotation prevents changing away from Converted. An active linked quote prevents declaring the opportunity Lost. The loss command requires at least one reason and stores reasons/competitors. Entering Lost dates the loss; leaving Lost clears the reasons under the manual transition hook. Do not derive a booked receivable or recognized revenue from a converted opportunity. ## Alternative quotation lines and partial sales orders

Read quotation rows in order. A main row immediately preceding an alternative starts a choice group; successive alternative rows belong to it until another main/ordinary row. For ordering-status purposes, a group contributes its actually ordered rows. If none were ordered, its main line still represents an unfulfilled promise. Ordinary rows remain independent. If no linked order quantities exist the result is Open. With at least one ordered row, every effective row must be present and cover its quoted stock quantity to reach Ordered. Otherwise it is Partially Ordered.

Without explicit selection, order mapping takes eligible non-alternative lines. With explicit selection, apply it to alternative sets, while still including other eligible simple lines. Eligibility uses remaining stock units or an unmapped eligible zero-quantity unit-price line. Copy max(quoted stock − ordered stock, 0) and divide by the quote line's conversion factor for commercial quantity. Preserve blanket-order identity and agreed rate if applicable.

Example: a quotation offers pump A or B plus a service visit. Ordering B satisfies the pump choice. It remains Partially Ordered until the service is also ordered. Quoting 3 boxes of 6 with 6 pieces already ordered leaves 12 stock units, or 2 boxes. Alternative A is never required in addition to B simply to close the quotation. ## Approve discounts by economic effect

The transaction-authorization rules have a basis (grand total, average discount, customer discount, item discount or item-group discount), company scope, threshold, subject user/role and authorized approver user/role. For document-wide average, sum each quantity times base list rate, falling back to base actual rate when list rate is absent/zero. Separately sum quantity times base actual rate and subtract document-level base discount. Average discount percentage = 100 − 100 × adjusted actual / list total. If the list denominator is zero the initialized average is zero. For 2 units listed at 100 and sold at 90, plus 1 unit listed and sold at 50, and a further 5 off the document, the weighted average discount is **10%** (225 versus 250). Averaging the two row percentages would incorrectly yield 5%.

For item and item-group bases, take the larger of stored row discount and the percentage implicit in actual rate against a nonzero list rate, rounding the implicit value to the discount field's precision. List 100 and actual 80 imply 20% even when the typed discount is zero. Customerwise discount on a quotation requires its party to be a Customer. Subject-specific rules are considered before role-specific and then general rules; the item and group bases can still require additional evaluations. At each applicable scope prefer company-specific threshold matches, then rules with no company. For item/group consider a matching named master before the blank-master fallback. Threshold selection accepts values up to and including the measured amount. Once crossed, an approver from the highest reached threshold **or a higher threshold above the measured amount** can qualify. A matching approver identity or role suffices for that check. Scope these rules separately from ordinary read/write/submit permissions. ## Count a coupon once across order and invoice

Gift cards require a customer match. Validate optional start/end against today's date inclusively and reject exhaustion of a nonzero usage limit. Counting a use increments even when the limit is unlimited. Cancellation decrements a positive counter. A return or consolidated sales invoice does not consume an additional use. A sales invoice for which **every** item links back to its actual submitted order row, already carrying the same coupon, customer and company, inherits the earlier use. A mixed invoice containing a standalone row or an incorrect order-to-row pairing consumes a use at invoice level under the observed decision. This is per commercial document, not a count per line. ## Interpret conversion reports

| Measure | Population, arithmetic and distinguishing case |
|---|---|
| Campaign efficiency | Group accessible leads with a campaign by lead creation time in the selected period (end inclusive by next-day exclusive boundary). Include later opportunities/quotes issued to these leads or to customers converted from them. Count submitted quotes. “Order count” counts quotations in Ordered or Partially Ordered status, so three orders from one quote count once. Order value sums submitted order lines' base net values linked via quote identity. Ratios use opportunity count / lead count, quote count / lead count and ordered-quote count / quote count times 100, with a denominator fallback of 1. Two leads, three opportunities, two quotes and one partially ordered quote yield 150%, 100%, 50%. |
| Lead conversion time | Iterate permitted opportunities originating from leads in creation order. Count a lead once after finding a qualifying row. Match the earliest submitted customer invoice in the reporting range **by contact email**. Count messages sent from that address up to invoice day. Duration in days is invoice posting date minus the earliest message sent *to* that exact parsed address. Count all issues raised by the address. A mean row averages these customer rows, unweighted by message count. Shared email can associate different contacts. |
| First response time for opportunities | Limit to permitted opportunities, optional company and creation date range. Group by creation date descending and average strictly positive recorded response durations. Rows with zero/missing duration do not contribute. A group containing 0, 60 and 180 seconds yields 120 seconds. |

A cohort can produce more than 100% opportunities per lead because these are document counts. A date filter on lead creation does not restrict all later sales evidence to that period. These reports cannot substitute for the revenue ledger.
