# Storefront, marketplace orders, carrier booking and receipts

## Check out a party's cart

A shop cart is a draft Quotation, selected by party, current contact email and Shopping Cart order type, taking the most recently modified matching draft. A Website Item supplies website presentation and the website warehouse, while Item owns stock identity. Setting a line to a nonzero quantity creates it or overwrites the first matching item quantity. Setting quantity to zero removes that item, deleting the quotation if it was the last line. Reapply current pricing, tax and shipping settings. An implementation must handle an empty cart explicitly in its response.

Place order assigns the Webshop company's identity and submits the quotation. It requires at least one shipping or billing address, maps to a sales order, clears the copied payment schedule, and, if out-of-stock items are disallowed, checks stock items in their website warehouse. Zero available stock rejects, as does requested quantity above available. Non-stock service lines bypass that stock test. Insert and submit the sales order, clear the cart count, return the order identity. The quotation can be submitted before the address/stock checks; specify and exercise transaction rollback on rejection. A cart snapshot is not a stock reservation. For five available units, two simultaneous requests for four require a reservation/fulfilment concurrency test.

Quotation-only checkout saves or submits the quote according to its own configuration. Shipping address ownership, customer/lead to customer conversion, cancellation, stock posting and invoice collection are separate boundaries. ## Import and unwind a Shopify order

A provider order identity is persisted on the ERP sales order. A callback finding an existing order records a duplicate/invalid integration result and returns before creation. Otherwise resolve the provider customer, create or refresh addresses where appropriate and resolve products. The integration uses the provider's order data for prices. If any referenced product no longer exists, a successful partial order cannot be assumed. A paid source order can generate an invoice and a fulfilled order can generate delivery notes via the corresponding handlers.

For each item let p be the supplied unit price, q the positive quantity, D the sum of line discounts and T the sum of line tax entries. The imported rate is p − D/q for exclusive taxes, and p − (D+T)/q for inclusive taxes. With p=60, q=2, D=10 and T=12, the exclusive rate is 55 and the inclusive rate is 49. For the inclusive line, 2×49 + 12 = 110, the customer's 120 list amount less the 10 discount. Rates at or below zero set the free-item flag and 100% discount to prevent automatic re-pricing. Separate tax rows use configured company accounts.

Cancellation looks up the original ERP order by external identity. If it has no invoice or delivery note and is submitted, cancel it. Otherwise attach the external financial status to the order and located downstream documents. The external cancellation alone does not unwind posted revenue, cash or stock. A refund needs its own financial event and reference. Serial re-delivery of a known order has a guard; concurrent duplicate delivery needs independent qualification. ## Quote and book carriage

Pass pickup/delivery addresses, parcels, service context and pickup date to enabled providers. Combine their offers, keeping the provider and service identities. The ordering key places offers with prices first, ascending by total price. Zero is a valid price, unknown is distinct. The booking service refuses an offer with a missing price. For a successful carrier booking persist provider, carrier, service, provider consignment identifier, price, waybill and Booked state on Shipment. Update the supplied linked delivery notes with returned shipment data. Provider booking, label attachment, tracking updates and inventory delivery are distinct effects. A carrier timeout after acceptance requires reconciling the provider consignment before retry; the inspected booking handler does not supply a transport-wide exactly-once guarantee. ## Capture a payment and settle a request

Provider settings own supported currency, minimum amount and credentials. The inspected Stripe charge adapter checks its supported currencies and minimum table. For USD a charge below 0.50 fails the minimum check; equality passes. That adapter sends the integer conversion of major units multiplied by 100. For USD 24.50 this is 2450. The multiplier is an adapter-specific finding. Currency exponent handling needs explicit tests per gateway. Create an integration request, mark it Completed on captured success, then call the linked business document's authorized-payment handler. Exceptions in that downstream notification are logged. A successful provider capture can therefore coexist with outstanding ERP settlement.

The ERP Payment Request handler accepts Authorized or Completed while status is not Paid. The phone-channel branch updates Paid and clears outstanding directly. The other branch creates a payment entry and may generate an invoice if requested. For an invoice in foreign currency with the party account in company currency, convert the party-currency outstanding to bank/request currency using the reference's exchange rate, at request grand-total precision. At 18 company units per request unit a party balance of 360 settles for 20 request units in this branch. Persist request, reference voucher and allocation identities so a bank reconciliation can locate the authoritative cash event. Verify provider event, request status and submitted accounting entry independently when recovering from partial failure.
