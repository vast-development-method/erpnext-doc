# Student billing, assessment and appointment accounting

## Convert an academic schedule to receivables

A fee structure defines the categories. A fee schedule selects the structure, program, academic year/term, student category, student groups, due date, posting date and component lines. Validation sums component totals to get the per-student schedule amount, refreshes counts for every group, then multiplies the sum of group counts by the per-student amount. Overlapping groups can therefore inflate the aggregate. A program mismatch, a category outside the structure and the structure total comparison use warnings. For the latter the code sums *stored* schedules by structure and compares that per-student sum to the structure amount. Treating all three messages as hard errors changes the operator's workflow.

On submission, the order/invoice setting determines the pending state. The generation command marks the schedule In Process. Up to ten students run synchronously, larger counts queue the work, zero students cause an error. Resolve each selected student's customer, creating missing customer details through the student helper. Map components to quantity-one items with the component amount as price list rate and component discount as a percentage. The invoice carries the schedule reference and student and uses the schedule dates as configured. For orders, the due date becomes delivery date. Auto-submission is a separate setting for each document type. Saving a draft invoice leaves the receivable unposted.

For three students with component totals 100 and 20, the schedule forecast is 360. If submitted linked student invoices total 240 with 80 outstanding, the dashboard reports 160 paid and 80 unpaid. The unissued 120 forms part of the forecast only. The generator catches per-student exceptions, attempts the remaining students, and on any error rolls back and records Failed and the error. It also publishes final progress 100 on that branch. Inspect the schedule state and committed documents for completion. A retry/duplicate-student case must inspect resulting invoice identities; progress alone cannot prove uniqueness. ## Score an assessment

Require student group membership. Resolve each criterion's maximum score from the assessment plan, reject a score above it, and apply the grading scale to each percentage. The overall percentage is 100 × sum of scores / record maximum score. Given scores of 18/20 and 30/60, the total is 48/80 or 60%. Averaging the two percentages would yield 70% and may yield a different grade. The duplicate check excludes the current record and cancelled results, comparing student and plan. Include zero maxima in failure qualification because division needs a defined denominator. ## Reserve an appointment

Patient Appointment owns patient, practitioner, service unit, date, start time, duration and state. For a timed practitioner booking, compare same-date appointments of the same patient **or** practitioner. Closed and Cancelled appointments are excluded. Strict interior overlaps and equal starts conflict; one appointment ending exactly when the next starts is allowed. With overlapping appointments enabled on a service unit, overlapping candidates for that unit and other patients may be admitted up to its capacity (default one). Candidates with the same patient still conflict. This query starts from the patient/practitioner candidate set, so it cannot serve as an all-practitioner unit occupancy query. The separate check-in mode tests an exact patient/date/time/appointment-for and check-in flag. Preserve its different test.

Date-derived open, scheduled and no-show states coexist with manually retained checked-in, checked-out, closed, cancelled and no-show states. A change of date must respect the state guards. Insurance coverage creation and fee validity depend on the invoice popup, free-follow-up policy and practitioner configuration. Calendar events and service-request progress are separate linked records. ## Bill and reverse an appointment

The billing operation uses the patient's linked customer, appointment company and that company's receivable account. It creates and submits a sales invoice with a line referencing the appointment. On the payment popup path, a nonzero percentage reduces the original paid amount by that percentage. A nonzero absolute discount then replaces the computed paid amount with original paid less the absolute discount. Both invoice discount attributes may be set; this precedence describes the payment-row calculation. For a paid amount of 200, 10% and an absolute amount of 12, the payment row is 188. With only the percentage, it is 180. A mode and nonzero original paid amount create an immediate POS payment row. Otherwise the invoice can be unpaid. Persist the resulting invoice identity, paid amount and invoiced flag on the appointment.

On practitioner appointment cancellation, reverse linked insurance coverage, reactivate a linked service request and update fee validity. For an invoiced appointment the automated cancellation helper checks the popup setting and a single invoice item. It cancels a submitted matching single-line invoice. A multiple-line invoice requires review, protecting other appointment charges. A linked calendar event moves to Cancelled. Operational access, clinical record confidentiality and the payment/claims boundary need separate permission tests. ## Consume an outpatient follow-up entitlement

A Fee Validity record owns the start date, last eligible date, creation timestamp, visited count and allowed maximum. Past end date wins the status decision, including when the visit count also equals the limit. On or before the end date, an exact count match yields Completed, otherwise the status helper sets Active. An overshoot is a distinct upstream edge to qualify. The date coverage helper allows a visit on or after start **or** on or after the validity record's creation date. This allows a same-day consultation to use a future-start entitlement created that day. A visit predating both fails that particular test.

Only an appointment or encounter may be a visit. The lookup requires patient read access and, for an already-saved visit, read access to the visit itself. Route appointments by appointment date and encounters by encounter date. An explicit inpatient link or an admitted patient inpatient record sends the visit down the inpatient billing path. Use this boundary when testing an operator who can see a patient but cannot read the requested encounter.
