## Publisher evidence

Indictrans Tech lists version 12. Staff record an incident at a location, attach a photo to the initial report (called FIR), and distribute that report according to internal assignment and escalation rules. The publisher names email and SMS, immediate remedies, a detailed report, a root-cause form, prevention measures and analytical reporting. The listing does not specify a statutory filing, deadline or escalation hierarchy. ## Related publisher deployment

Indictrans describes a customized Knight Frank India deployment. Its First Information Report links site code, client and incident reference to three milestones. The 24-hour stage captures initial facts, injury evidence and immediate action. The 48-hour stage records severity and likelihood of actual and potential harm to people, assets, community and environment. The 30-day stage follows corrective actions toward closure. Notification recipients include internal and client contacts chosen using site conditions. These intervals are evidence for that customer's configuration; the case study does not identify a marketplace build.
