# AQL Quality Assurance By HavaraTech

The publisher lists Frappe versions 15 and 16 and a nightly channel. It describes incoming, in-process, transfer and outgoing inspections linked to Purchase Receipts, Job Cards, Stock Entries and Delivery Notes. Item templates define parameters, classifications and required readings. The published workflow derives sample size from a lot and inspection level, records results per sample and decides whether to release a lot. [Marketplace listing](https://cloud.frappe.io/marketplace/apps/aql_quality_assurance) · [HavaraTech product guide](https://havaratech.com/aql-quality-assurance/)

## What the recovered source implements

A public copy under BABLU855 carries the marketplace app identifier, HavaraTech author and publisher contact. Commit `2e4b84826934b73cf133d6b8cfe92c22c67cb541` is a reference snapshot. Its relationship to the currently distributed package remains unknown. [Package identity](https://github.com/BABLU855/aql_quality_assurance/blob/2e4b84826934b73cf133d6b8cfe92c22c67cb541/pyproject.toml)

- The source registers controller replacements for Quality Inspection and Purchase Receipt. The inspection controller resolves each valid parameter from party, then item, then global settings. It groups quantities for matching item codes on the reference transaction. For stock movements it reads transfer quantity with a row quantity fallback. The lot is converted to an integer. Lot band and inspection level select a sample size, and each severity scale supplies an acceptance limit. [Controller registration](https://github.com/BABLU855/aql_quality_assurance/blob/2e4b84826934b73cf133d6b8cfe92c22c67cb541/aql_quality_assurance/hooks.py#L193) · [Parameter and table logic](https://github.com/BABLU855/aql_quality_assurance/blob/2e4b84826934b73cf133d6b8cfe92c22c67cb541/aql_quality_assurance/aql_quality_assurance_by_havaratech/doctype/quality_inspection/quality_inspection.py#L849)
- It counts rejected **reading rows** by critical, major and minor class. Exceeding any class limit rejects the lot. Even within those limits the final decision checks, in order, for on-hold, pending and rejected rows. A completed sample containing one rejected reading can therefore finish rejected even when the numerical threshold permits one. Strict completion, early-rejection, skip and manual-override branches affect submission separately. [Decision](https://github.com/BABLU855/aql_quality_assurance/blob/2e4b84826934b73cf133d6b8cfe92c22c67cb541/aql_quality_assurance/aql_quality_assurance_by_havaratech/doctype/quality_inspection/quality_inspection.py#L289) · [Submission](https://github.com/BABLU855/aql_quality_assurance/blob/2e4b84826934b73cf133d6b8cfe92c22c67cb541/aql_quality_assurance/aql_quality_assurance_by_havaratech/doctype/quality_inspection/quality_inspection.py#L10)
- The receipt controller converts a linked rejected inspection’s received quantity into rejected quantity with zero accepted quantity. If needed it copies the row warehouse and batch onto the rejected side. Its submission check requires each row to have an existing submitted inspection and permits a rejected inspection. This documents a receipt into rejected stock. The other advertised transaction gates require their own replay. [Receipt controller](https://github.com/BABLU855/aql_quality_assurance/blob/2e4b84826934b73cf133d6b8cfe92c22c67cb541/aql_quality_assurance/aql_quality_assurance_by_havaratech/doctype/purchase_receipt/purchase_receipt.py#L13)

## Proposed rebuild contract and acceptance

Record the source document line, item, lot, unit, inspection stage, plan/table version, level, limits, parameter and sample number for each observation. Store the table decision separately from execution status and override disposition. A supervisor’s exception carries an actor, time and reason. A stage action must distinguish releasing saleable stock, accepting a receipt into a rejected warehouse and halting shipment.

| Fixture | Expected observation |
|---|---|
| All required readings accepted for a completed inspection | Eligible for release under the configured gate. Report the captured plan and count zero rejected rows in each class. |
| Major limit 1, one rejected major reading, all other readings accepted, no override | The recovered controller yields rejected. A rebuild implementing threshold-only sampling must name that policy explicitly and return its different outcome in this fixture. |
| Receipt for 12 units linked to a submitted rejected inspection | Under the recovered receipt disposition the line has 0 accepted and 12 rejected. A draft inspection cannot satisfy the submission check. |

Translate inspector prompts, defect descriptions, dispositions and customer certificate captions. Keep numeric readings, AQL scales, inspection-level codes, sample numbers and identifiers invariant. A German certificate and an English inspection screen must report the same counts and disposition. Record the language of free-text observations. Localised status captions must never be compared by the decision engine. Public materials and static source do not establish certification or runtime equivalence.
