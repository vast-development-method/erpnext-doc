# ERP business areas and coverage

A purchase, stock movement or invoice can change several ledgers. Start with the business event below, follow its workflow and use the linked lifecycle catalog to identify the participating records. The [entity dictionary](../../../schema/README.md) supplies field values, currencies, child ownership and grants. The [service catalogs](../../services/README.md) cover operations that cross business areas.

## Reconcile a decision

- For a delivery billed at a changed price, resolve the source row, quantity and capped billing basis in the [stock-controller decisions](../checkpoint-corrections/README.md).
- For project costing, [follow labour, direct purchasing and signed stock changes](../../transactions/project-costing.md). The [controlled reference comparison](project-costing.md#isolated-controller-comparison) records the effects of selecting different project-costing controllers with the same currency inputs.
- For physical and financial posting, [trace twelve vouchers and six financial reports](../live/README.md). The [warehouse fulfilment run](../warehouse-fulfilment/README.md), [platform observations](../platform-live/README.md) and [124 workflow test cases](reference-tests.md) identify the operations and outcomes that were exercised.
- For an independent implementation, apply the [qualification criteria](../qualification.json) to the actual company, effective settings, enabled capabilities, actor and supported languages.

## Evidence behind the catalogs

The [declaration comparison](declaration-observations.md) preserves per-area record, field, report, exposed operation and function counts, source line locations, and the census method. The [entity cross-reference](entity-cross-reference.tsv) resolves each source declaration to its business identifier and dictionary file. Use those references to investigate a particular declaration or to interpret imported data. A stored historic field does not enable a corresponding workflow. The [catalogs of optional capabilities](../marketplace-workflows/README.md) distinguish reviewed code, declared interfaces and documented descriptions.
