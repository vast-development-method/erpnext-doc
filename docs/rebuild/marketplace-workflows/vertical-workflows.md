# Farm stock, piece-rate payroll and incoming material prices

A farm activity, a payroll calculation and a price warning can all change a later financial result. The three extensions below place those decisions at different points in the document lifecycle. Each trace describes a particular publisher revision. The acceptance examples specify what a replacement should demonstrate. No extension was installed for these traces.

## Livestock Management • from daily capture to stock valuation

A worker records the company, employee, animal item, batch, house and activity time. The quick-entry form supports enabled batch-tracked livestock without serial numbers. It checks the employee's company and active status, the batch's item, the warehouse's company and the available population **at the activity time**. Future activity, an activity before batch manufacture and an empty house at that time are rejected. A project, if present, must belong to the company. The target record depends on the activity. [Context and activity types][farm-context]

| Activity | Document created | Data that controls its effect |
|---|---|---|
| Feeding | Stock Entry, Material Issue | Each row issues a positive stock-UOM quantity of feed from its source warehouse. Feed item and livestock item must differ. |
| Mortality | Stock Entry, Material Issue | A readable mortality reason and whole positive count are required. The count cannot exceed this batch's population in this house. The stock row issues livestock from that house. |
| Produce collection | Stock Entry, Material Receipt | Each row receives a different stock item into the specified warehouse at a positive, manually supplied basic rate. |
| Weighing | Weight Record | The whole sample size cannot exceed the population. The previous measurement is selected for the same batch, item, company, house and kilogram unit, before the observation, excluding cancelled records. |
| Husbandry observation | Livestock Event | Requires a readable husbandry practice and an end time on or after the start. Pregnancy checks use the dedicated structured form. |

The stock-entry type must have the expected purpose. Feed and produce accept one to 100 rows of enabled, non-serialized stock items, with an enabled matching inventory batch where required. All five requests **create a document**. Saving a farm activity does not submit a stock movement. The response carries the document type, name, modification time, document status and workflow status when configured. [Document mapping][farm-docs] · [Stock row validation][farm-rows] · [Response and save lifecycle][farm-save]

**Retry contract.** Supply a UUID per operation and keep the original payload for retries. The publisher hashes the canonicalized JSON with sorted object keys, then looks up that operation within the destination record type. A matching request returns the previously created record after a read-permission check. Changing the payload for that same identifier and type raises an error. Concurrent duplicate insertions roll back to a savepoint and read the winning row. Because several activities share Stock Entry as their destination, a UUID reused between feeding and mortality can conflict. The lookup happens before revalidating current stock, which permits recovery of a lost response after the house population changes. [Identity, fingerprint and duplicate recovery][farm-save]

**Cost flow on submission.** For supported activity types the before-save handler sets the row expense account from the company when configured. Submitting a Feeding or Treatment material issue with rows and an active capitalization batch creates a linked Repack. The repack consumes and receives the same livestock, in the same warehouse and quantity, at the source posting time. It carries the actual issued amounts, grouped by expense account, as additional costs. ERP stock valuation determines the value of the consumed animals. An empty capitalization warehouse population prevents this revaluation. Mortality and produce collection do not enter this particular submit handler's capitalization branch. [Wiring][farm-hooks] · [Accounts and eligibility][farm-cap] · [Quantity, amounts and submitted repack][farm-repack]

**Worked acceptance.** Put 20 batch-tracked animals valued at NAD 2,000 in HOUSE-A. Enter NAD 120 of feed consumption with capitalization active and no other costs, movement or rounding difference. Saving produces one draft and leaves animal stock unchanged. Submit once and reconcile the feed issue and linked repack. Feed stock value falls by NAD 120, the animal count stays at 20 and its aggregate value becomes NAD 2,120, or NAD 106 each. For a mortality capture, quantity 21 fails against the same available population of 20. Repeat the original save from an Afrikaans interface using the original UUID, activity code, payload and document-type identity. The same record must be returned. Test cancellation of the posted pair independently and reconcile both stock and ledger on a fresh read. The cited hook map exposes before-save and submit for Stock Entry; the exact cancellation interaction needs installation evidence.

## Piece Rate Pay • from production quantities to salary structure assignments

A daily payout binds an attendance date and shift to activity/item quantities. The server retrieves a costing rate by the activity and item pair and rejects a missing rate. The form multiplies rate by quantity and sums the line amounts. Choosing an item initializes its quantity to one. The calculation's fallback also substitutes one for a zero quantity while the quantity total sums zero, so entering zero can produce a non-zero amount. The server validation shown checks for an attendance date and shift. [Payout validation and rate][pay-service] · [Amount and quantity calculation][pay-lines]

**Who shares the pool.** Fetching workers queries Attendance by date, shift and company, then takes the first returned matching IN and OUT check-in per attendance, if present. The query retrieves attendance status but does not restrict it and specifies no ordering for multiple check-ins. A refresh replaces the employee rows. Calculating payout divides the total equally among the resulting rows, independently of the hours between the displayed timestamps. With no rows it shows a message. [Attendance lookup][pay-attendance] · [Roster replacement and distribution][pay-split]

**When a daily amount is consumed.** The weekly Calculate Payout action reads submitted, unprocessed daily payouts within the inclusive date interval. It groups employee-row amounts by employee identifier and marks each source with employee rows processed **during calculation**. The input query is scoped by dates and flags, without a company or weekly-assignment identifier. The form replaces its current weekly employee rows with the result. On weekly submission, each employee row creates a Salary Structure Assignment with the selected salary structure, its base equal to the employee's total, effective from the end date, and a back-reference to the weekly document. This step writes a payroll basis. The reviewed handlers do not issue a bank payment. On cancellation, the date-range handler clears processed flags for submitted, processed payouts in the interval, without checking their owning assignment. The shown cancellation hook does not cancel the salary assignments it created. [Weekly selection and flags][pay-week] · [Form handling][pay-week-ui] · [Assignment and cancellation][pay-assignment]

**Worked acceptance.** In one company a crew produces 12 units at NAD 5 and four units at NAD 10, giving a daily pool of NAD 100. Two employee rows receive NAD 50 each; unequal check-in durations do not change this result. A second submitted payout gives the first employee NAD 30. Aggregating these two daily payouts produces assignment bases of NAD 80 and NAD 50. A rebuild should store the company, currency, source payout IDs and employee IDs on the allocation. Reopening the preview should reproduce the amounts. Submitting it twice must produce one set of assignments. Cancelling it should release its own payouts and reconcile its own generated assignments, including when another company's weekly run has identical dates. These ownership and replay requirements address the source's date-range behavior.

Rerun in German using translated activity and shift labels with the same identifiers. Also split NAD 100 among three eligible employees. For a two-decimal policy that awards the residual cent by ascending employee ID, expect NAD 33.34, 33.33 and 33.33 in that order in every language. This is a specified replacement policy; the source form divides the amount without allocating a rounding residual. Define whether a zero-quantity line should be rejected or earn zero and enforce the chosen policy at submission.

## Material Price Control • selecting a rule and deciding whether to submit

The extension attaches its checks to **before submit** on five voucher types. An enabled guard checks positive purchase order quantities against the row's order rate, and purchase receipts and stock-updating purchase invoices against valuation rate. Purchase invoice without stock update is skipped. Internal suppliers can be excluded. For stock entries it checks positive incoming transfers to a target warehouse except the Material Transfer and Material Transfer for Manufacture purposes. Positive reconciliation quantities use the row valuation rate. Each path skips a zero rate. [Lifecycle hooks][price-hooks] · [Purchase inputs][price-in] · [Stock inputs][price-stock]

**Rule identity and precedence.** Resolve warehouse from the row's warehouse, target warehouse or the document's default warehouse, in that order. Choose posting date, then transaction date, then today. Search enabled rules for the exact item before trying its direct item group. Within either search prefer a matching dated warehouse rule, a dated warehouse-independent rule, an undated warehouse rule, then an undated warehouse-independent rule. Bounds are inclusive and can be open-ended. Thus an undated item rule outranks even a dated group rule. The rule editor rejects overlapping enabled dated rules and duplicate enabled perpetual rules for the same target and warehouse. A dated override can coexist with a perpetual fallback. [Context and missing rule][price-context] · [Resolution and inclusive bounds][price-rule] · [Rule authoring][price-author]

**Decision and writes.** The variance percentage is the absolute difference between incoming and expected rate, divided by expected rate, times 100. The helper returns zero for a zero expected rate. Use the rule's nonzero allowance, otherwise the settings default. The severe threshold is allowance times severe multiplier. A rate strictly outside an active hard minimum/maximum is severe before the percentage test. Otherwise a variance strictly above the severe threshold is severe; strictly above the allowance is a warning. An anomaly attempts to insert an Open log identifying voucher, warehouse, item, rates, variance and severity. Severe blocking requires a configured authorized role and nonblank override reason to proceed. On a successful override the handler stamps the user, time and reason in the log, and the user and time on the voucher. A missing-rule block is checked separately using the configured bypass roles. An insert preceding an exception does not establish that the log survived transaction rollback. [Thresholds and override][price-decision] · [Variance and log fields][price-log] · [Role match][price-role]

| Comparable incoming rate, expected 100 | Allowance 10%, multiplier 2, no hard bounds |
|---:|---|
| 90 or 110 | 10% exactly. Accept. |
| 80 or 120 | 20% exactly. Warning, because it exceeds 10% but does not exceed 20%. |
| 79 or 121 | 21%. Severe. A blocking configuration requires an authorized override and reason. |
| 116, with a separate hard maximum of 115 | Severe from the bound, even though percentage variance is 16%. |

**Worked acceptance.** For item MAT-A configure a dated group rate of 95 for WH-A, a perpetual item rate of 110 for all warehouses and an item rate of 100 for WH-A valid through 30 June. On 30 June, a receipt in WH-A at 121 selects the 100 rule and returns severe. On 1 July the same receipt selects 110, giving 10% and passing at a 10% allowance. In both cases capture the selected rule ID, comparable amount, currency and stock unit in the rebuilt audit record. Repeat in English and German with the same voucher and rate. Some upstream validation messages embed a rupee symbol; the rebuild must render the applicable currency independently of language. When a purchase order uses another currency or UOM, convert its unit price to the rule's currency and unit on the applicable date before comparing, and persist the conversion basis. The publisher compares the purchase order's row rate directly. This normalization is a replacement decision. [Order rate input][price-in] · [Publisher message formatting][price-message]
