# Count adjustments and stock closing

## Count stock as of the posting instant

A reconciliation owns a company, timestamp, purpose, difference account, cost centre and identified item/location rows. A row can capture a target quantity, valuation rate, serial or batch selection, inventory dimensions and evidence of the prior balance. Interpret a target of 7 as a closing count. If the ledger held 10, the quantity effect is −3. Compute amount change by rounding each extended value to amount precision before subtraction. Ten units at 12.50 replaced by seven units at 14.00 change stock by **−27.00**, even though the new unit rate is higher. In a perpetual-inventory company the resulting simple entry debits stock adjustment 27 and credits inventory 27.

| Input or condition | Required decision |
|---|---|
| No effective change | Drop unchanged ordinary rows. If all rows drop, reject an empty change. Comparing rates uses rate precision. A saved identity bundle follows its own comparison path. |
| Quantity or rate omitted | Reuse the historical balance when available. With a positive count and a still-missing rate, try historical stock valuation, a buying price in default currency, then the item's valuation. Explicit zero is an entered revaluation. Positive quantity at zero requires the zero-rate allowance in the ordinary untracked posting path. |
| Duplicate or invalid count | Check item/location plus supplied serial, batch and dimension values on each row. Reject a negative count or rate, fractional indivisible units, serial identifiers against zero quantity and invalid inventory dimensions. |
| Opening stock | Reject an item/location with an earlier effective stock posting through the chosen timestamp matching all specified dimensions. A perpetual opening, including the company's first stock posting under this guard, rejects a profit-and-loss difference account. A stock-type difference account is disallowed. |
| Active reservations | Quantity-changing rows enter the reservation check on submit and cancel. A value-only ordinary row with equal target and current quantity does not enter that check. Under a report-generated adjustment, net the row quantity changes by item/location first. Tracked identity and future batch balance constraints can still block a change. |
| Empty lot with residual warehouse value | Resolve whole item/location quantity first. A lot with zero stock does not permit a write-off of the other lots' value. When the entire location is at zero quantity, a material stranded value is written off with zero units and the previous running rate carried forward. |

Submission writes movements, checks subsequent batch sufficiency, writes financial entries and requests revaluation of dependent future entries. Cancellation checks reservations, reverses its own entries and financial effects, triggers dependent revaluation and removes qualifying automatically created batches. Distinguish a historical-time stock lookup from today's balance when drafting or retrying. ## Correct valuation layers from a comparison

A distinct system-generated purpose corrects the stored valuation state. Require origin in the valuation comparison and recompute the complete plan for each affected item/location at the selected time. Reject incomplete, stale or edited lot/quantity/rate sets. Compare rates at the configured valuation-rate precision and retain the plan's original layer order when generating rows. Only the first row of each group carries the ledger's existing quantity and value in the before-columns.

For a tracked item, establish removable lot balances (including required zero-value priming movements), count out prior holdings, then count in the planned quantities at their specified rates and identities. Validate the final summed quantity to tolerance 0.000001 and value to 0.01 × (number of plan rows + 1). For a three-row plan the value tolerance is 0.04. A discrepancy of 0.05 fails. A previously recorded 9 units worth 89 replaced by layers 2 × 9, 3 × 10 and 4 × 11 still represents 9 units, now worth 92. The report presents a net value change of +3 and zero net quantity. On cancellation undo reset movements in reverse order. A later submitted adjustment can block an earlier posting or cancellation affecting its item/location or overlapping tracked lots. ## Publish a closing balance

A closing is a derived stock snapshot used to start subsequent reporting. Reject future cutoffs. The first period starts at the earliest effective stock posting for the company, or at the cutoff if there is no earlier one. The next starts the day after the latest submitted cutoff and must end later. Submission requires effective document authority. Explicit regeneration requires write access, a submitted closing, no later submitted closing depending on it, and no covering accounting period close. The same dependency/closed-period constraints govern cancellation.

| Phase | Decision and durable effect |
|---|---|
| Queue | Require submission and absence of pending company reposts at or before cutoff. Mark in progress and schedule work after the surrounding transaction commits. |
| Build | Recheck submission and pending reposts at worker entry. Carry the previous usable close plus subsequent ledger changes. A lot join can repeat one ledger entry; at item/location grain count its total value once. At finer batch/dimension grain use the split values when present. Skip rows with both zero quantity and zero value. Retain zero-quantity residual-value rows with rate zero. |
| Publish | Re-read submission under lock. A cancellation during computation removes generated rows and ends Cancelled. Coordinate with postings under the company lock. If a through-cutoff ledger entry changed during generation, or an eligible repost is now pending, publish Outdated instead of Completed. Failure has a distinct status. |
| Later activity | A backdated posting can mark existing completed closings covering its effective date Outdated. A current closing for financial-period use must be completed and unscoped, with cutoff no later than the closed accounting period. Regeneration removes old generated balances before queueing the rebuild. |

A receipt of 12 units worth 96 split into batches A=4/32 and B=8/64 gives one location total 12/96 at rate 8. A backdated receipt of 2/18 posted after that closing makes it stale. An eligible regenerated close gives 14/114, with rate determined from 114/14 at effective precision. Report opening balances from the snapshot plus only movements after its cutoff. Summing its own source movements again doubles inventory.
