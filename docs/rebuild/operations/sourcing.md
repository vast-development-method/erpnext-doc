# Competitive sourcing and supplier standing

## Record an invited supplier's response

The buyer creates one request with item rows and invited suppliers. Duplicate suppliers, disabled or frozen parties, and a supplier whose standing prevents requests fail validation. A warning flag surfaces a caution; the buyer can proceed. For the portal command, accept the request identifier, supplier identity, quoted row identifiers with quantity and optional rate, terms, currency and buying price list. Return the newly saved supplier quotation identifier. It is a draft available for buyer review. Saving the quotation causes no stock or ledger posting.

| Decision | Observable rule |
|---|---|
| Who can respond | The signed-in user must belong to the selected supplier's portal users. Load the submitted request and confirm this supplier appears among its invitees. |
| Concurrency | Lock the request while checking active quotations for this supplier via their item links to this request. Draft and submitted quotations occupy the slot. A second request fails with the existing quotation. A cancelled response no longer occupies it. |
| Ownership | Each submitted row identifier must exist on that particular request. Source item, description, warehouse, UOM, conversion, material-request references, cost centre and accounting dimensions from the stored row. Look up the supplier part number for that item and supplier. |
| Pricing | Use the provided currency or the supplier's company-specific account currency, and the quoted price list or buying default. Round supplied rate to the quotation rate precision and preserve the supplier price against buying pricing rules. An explicit rate rounding to zero means a full discount. An omitted or empty rate permits normal default pricing. |
| Outcome | Only after these checks may the application create the draft under the internal creation capability. A rejected response cannot consume the slot or alter the request items. |

For two invited suppliers bidding for the same eight cases of twelve, each may create one linked quotation. A's explicit zero is preserved when the list rate is 30. B's omitted rate can pick up 30 from the applicable list under ordinary defaults. Neither can change a case's conversion to six by sending it back on the response. When one user's contact has several supplier links, the requested supplier must still pass both membership checks. ## Score a closed assessment period

A scorecard belongs to one supplier. A period snapshots the criterion references, formulas, weights, maximum scores, measured variables and inclusive assessment dates. Reject date reversal and overlap with a submitted period on the same scorecard, including a shared boundary day. Aggregate weights rounded to two places must equal 100. Resolve each configured variable, substitute its value rounded to two decimals (zero for a false value), and evaluate criteria in a restricted numeric expression context with minimum and maximum functions. Invalid expressions fail the assessment. A replacement may implement the permitted variable providers with any technology, while configuration of a measurement provider must remain a privileged action.

For criterion *i* with positive maximum *m*, weight *w* and expression result *r*, the contribution is *w* × min(*m*, max(0, *r*)) / *m*. A zero maximum contributes zero. Sum the contributions for period score. With 60% weight on quality and 40% on delivery, quality 92 out of 100 and delivery 115 out of 100 produce 55.2 + 40 = **95.2**. Only submitted periods enter the supplier score, ordered by descending end date. Number the most recent period zero. Sum the configured weighting expression evaluated with actual scores and divide by its sum with every score replaced by 100. Multiply by 100 and round to one decimal place. If the total maximum weight is nonpositive, default to 100. For weighting score / (period number + 1) and two scores 95.2 then 80, the supplier earns **90.1**.

Standing intervals cover the score range 0–100 without overlaps or gaps. Lower bounds are inclusive; the highest finite upper bound is inclusive, other finite upper bounds are exclusive. Apply the selected colour, status, notification recipients and warning/prevention flags to the card and relevant supplier fields. Deleting the card clears the supplier's standing flags. The purchase-order guard checks for a standing as well as the prevent flag, whereas the request guard throws whenever its prevent flag is set. Under warning-only controls both operations show caution. A standing change notifies configured recipients when a sending account is available.

Period refresh requires write access to the supplier. Start at supplier creation, compute seven-day intervals, month ends, or one year minus one day. Generate completed periods only (end strictly before today's date). Skip ahead to the day after an overlapping submitted period. The batch isolates failures per scorecard. With weekly periods starting 1 May, the 1–7 May period is eligible on 8 May. Running again that day leaves one submitted period.
