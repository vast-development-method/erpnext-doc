# Picking for delivery, transfer and production

Store the source order row and pick-location row as independent keys. Several warehouses can satisfy one order row. Several rows in a bundle can refer to the same component. The quantity the customer ordered, the stock units the operator picked, the units reserved and the quantity delivered each have a different update event.

## Location and quantity decisions

| Step | Decision and observable effect |
|---|---|
| Resolve units | A stock-unit row has factor one. For a non-bundle linked order/request row, prefer the source row's conversion when the row ID, item and unit match. Otherwise use the item/unit master when present. Round quantity times factor to the pick row's stock precision. Apply integer-unit checks to transaction quantity and stock/picked quantities under their respective units. |
| Select locations | Automatic selection may split a row across warehouses. For a delivery, prefer the order warehouse and omit transit warehouses. For a manufacturing pick, omit the work-in-progress destination. Preserve the serial and batch identities with their source rows. |
| Admit a pick | For a row with a batch, compare quantity to pick with available batch stock at that warehouse. Otherwise compare with actual bin stock. In scan mode, reject a row whose picked quantity is short on submission. Outside scan mode, a missing/zero picked quantity defaults to the required stock quantity before submission. When a positive tracking selection exists its absolute selected quantity must agree with picked quantity at row precision. |
| Allocate to work | Lock the work order when checking the pending requirement. For a live material-request row use requested stock units less already ordered units and decrement that remainder across input rows. Pool other incoming rows by item and check against the work order's pending material demand. |
| Submit or cancel | Submission updates reference, bundle, sales-order picking and work-order quantities. Explicit reservation is a separate operation. Cancellation cancels open stock reservations sourced from this pick, updates the same progress measures and handles the linked tracking bundles. A post-submit edit is refused while the pick has reserved stock. |

## Delivery generation and partial fulfilment

The order-linked delivery service reads the pick, checks access and validates locations. Group sales orders only when customer, address fields, currency, price list, tax template, discount basis/rates/amounts, terms and ordered tax-row signatures match. Each tax-row signature includes charge type, account, rate, reference row, tax inclusion and cost centre. A fixed discount without a percentage or an actual-amount tax keeps that order in its own group. In the order-linked path each eligible group produces a saved draft. The sales-invoice target sets the stock-update flag. A pick containing demand without an order also has its own mapping path. One result can be returned directly. Multiple results are reported through document links. A pick with nothing remaining raises a business rejection. Draft creation and final voucher submission are distinct operations. [Grouping and eligibility](https://github.com/frappe/erpnext/blob/886ab6ae187244e7f1b852393384ab6019d56264/erpnext/stock/doctype/pick_list/pick_list.py#L1689-L1789) · [Persisting group drafts](https://github.com/frappe/erpnext/blob/886ab6ae187244e7f1b852393384ab6019d56264/erpnext/stock/doctype/pick_list/pick_list.py#L1903-L1924)

For an ordinary row, subtract delivered stock units from picked units. Convert the remaining units back into the target transaction unit. For a partly moved serial row, use its submitted voucher rows to identify serials already moved. Filter those identities from the original selection in pick order and cap the list at the remaining quantity. This works even if the serials moved differ from the first entries in the recorded selection. A separate transfer mapper uses picked less transferred quantity. It chooses the job-card mapping first when the material request has one, otherwise work order, material request or a reference-free transfer. [Serial readback](https://github.com/frappe/erpnext/blob/886ab6ae187244e7f1b852393384ab6019d56264/erpnext/stock/doctype/pick_list/pick_list.py#L1977-L2060) · [Transfer targets](https://github.com/frappe/erpnext/blob/886ab6ae187244e7f1b852393384ab6019d56264/erpnext/stock/doctype/pick_list/pick_list.py#L2128-L2160) · [Remaining transfer quantity](https://github.com/frappe/erpnext/blob/886ab6ae187244e7f1b852393384ab6019d56264/erpnext/stock/doctype/pick_list/pick_list.py#L2371-L2392)

For a product bundle, preserve each packed component row. To calculate picked parent units for the order row, sum picked stock units by component item across its packed rows. Divide by each component's total requirement per bundle, retaining whole bundles, and take the smallest complete count. For delivery from a particular pick, calculate the complete units from the relevant pending quantities. Sales-order progress across picks and the quantity deliverable from this pick answer different questions. [Order-level bundle calculation](https://github.com/frappe/erpnext/blob/886ab6ae187244e7f1b852393384ab6019d56264/erpnext/stock/doctype/pick_list/pick_list.py#L913-L929) · [Pending bundle calculation](https://github.com/frappe/erpnext/blob/886ab6ae187244e7f1b852393384ab6019d56264/erpnext/stock/doctype/pick_list/pick_list.py#L1092-L1106)

**Executed examples.** A bundle uses the same component on two rows, one and two units respectively. Ordering three bundles produces pick requirements of three and six. Picking all nine updates the order by three bundles and produces a three-bundle delivery.
