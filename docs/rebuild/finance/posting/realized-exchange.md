# Realized exchange difference on settlement

Keep the invoice or journal's booked party-account exchange rate, the payment's party-side rate, its bank-side rate and the allocated foreign amount. Store rates with units of company currency per foreign currency. Re-read the referenced record when a saved draft changes its allocation target. When the payment receives from the party use its source rate. When it pays the party use its target rate. Apply this to the ordinary referenced-invoice settlement path; advances in a separate account, internal transfers and unallocated payments have additional branches. [Rate resolution on the payment](https://github.com/frappe/erpnext/blob/886ab6ae187244e7f1b852393384ab6019d56264/erpnext/accounts/doctype/payment_entry/payment_entry.py#L1213-L1279) · [Changing a draft's reference](https://github.com/frappe/erpnext/blob/886ab6ae187244e7f1b852393384ab6019d56264/erpnext/accounts/doctype/payment_entry/test_payment_entry.py#L449-L574)

Compute `settled company value = round(allocated foreign × party-side payment rate)` and `booked company value = round(allocated foreign × reference rate)` at company payment-amount precision. The reference's exchange difference is the first less the second. Rounding each product before subtraction matters for partial and fractional allocations. The calculated difference may coexist with zero residual payment difference and zero unallocated foreign amount. Preserve separate voucher identities for the payment and the realized exchange journal.

For a receivable of 100.00 USD booked at 50.00 and received at 55.00 with matching foreign bank and party-side rates, the invoice recorded a base receivable of 5,000.00. The payment debits bank 5,500.00 and credits receivables 5,500.00. Its linked exchange journal debits receivables 500.00 and credits exchange gain 500.00. The foreign claim and base claim are both zero. For a supplier payable of the same size and rate movement, the payment debits payable and credits bank by 5,500.00. The exchange journal debits exchange loss and credits payable by 500.00. A fall in the settlement rate reverses the gain/loss direction. With equal rates no reference exchange journal is needed. For a partial receipt of 40.00 USD against a 100.00 USD claim booked at 50.00, a 48.00 settlement rate gives company receipt 1,920.00 and a −80.00 reference difference. Debit exchange loss 80.00 and credit receivables 80.00 in the linked exchange journal. The remaining foreign claim is 60.00 USD and its base carrying value is 3,000.00. A gain/loss adjustment in company currency must leave the foreign principal untouched. The exchange builder first examines already submitted exchange journals linked to the payment and referenced invoice/type/row index, skipping an existing leg for that reference. Cancellation follows the linked exchange journals. This source-side existence check does not itself establish concurrent exactly-once delivery.
