# Receivables financing and collection routing

A pledged invoice remains owed by the customer while the lender funds the business. Record the financed invoice amount, the customer claim and the short-term loan separately. The facility record stores company, loan dates, bank, charge expense, loan account, three receivables control accounts, and invoice rows with customer and pledged outstanding. Each resulting journal links its loan-account row to the facility. Each sanction reclassification carries the customer, invoice and applicable dimensions. [Facility definition](../../../schemas/data/record-types/invoice_discounting.json) · [User manual](https://docs.frappe.io/erpnext/invoice_discounting)

## Selection and authorization

The selection service requires a company, read access to that company and the discounting type, and permission-filtered access to the candidate invoices. Return submitted invoices in that company with outstanding greater than zero, excluding invoices already referenced by any submitted discounting row. Optional customer and inclusive posting date limits filter the result. Optional minimum and maximum amounts test **base grand total**. Thus a 1,000.00 invoice with 300.00 left can pass a 900.00 minimum. Return invoice identity, customer, date, outstanding and receivable account. On facility validation independently refuse an already pledged invoice or a proposed row greater than its live outstanding. The checks as written do not establish concurrent uniqueness between two new submissions. A replacement requiring that invariant should reserve the invoice and post the facility in one transaction. [Selection and routing](https://github.com/frappe/erpnext/blob/886ab6ae187244e7f1b852393384ab6019d56264/erpnext/accounts/doctype/invoice_discounting/invoice_discounting.py#L318-L376)

## Journal and state contract

| Operation | Acceptance and output | Posting effect |
|---|---|---|
| Submit the facility | Supply start date and nonzero loan period. Set end date to start plus the period in calendar days. Total the pledged rows. Set `Sanctioned` and flag linked invoices. | For each nonzero row debit sanctioned receivables and credit original trade receivables. Pass the invoice reference and disable outstanding update. |
| Prepare disbursement | Produce a draft journal referencing the facility. | On journal submission debit bank for pledged total less bank charges, debit charge expense for the charges, credit short-term loan for total. For each row debit discounted receivables and credit sanctioned receivables. |
| Receive from customer | While `Disbursed`, route the linked invoice collection to discounted receivables. While `Settled`, the routing helper chooses unpaid discounted receivables. Other states produce no account override. | Customer receipt reduces the outstanding invoice. The financing disbursement itself leaves the invoice outstanding. |
| Prepare settlement | Produce a draft journal. Always include principal repayment. Compute the date test against **today at preparation**, independently of the future journal's posting date. | On submission debit short-term loan and credit bank for the facility total. If end date is **strictly later** than today, credit discounted receivables and debit unpaid discounted receivables for each positive live invoice balance. On and after end date omit these additional rows. |
| Cancel journals | Cancellation of a settlement loan debit requires `Settled` and restores `Disbursed`. Cancellation of a disbursement loan credit requires `Disbursed` and restores `Sanctioned`. | Use the respective journal reversal. Cancelling the facility separately reverses its own classification and recomputes invoice flags from surviving submitted rows. |

The journal updates state from the referenced short-term-loan row. A positive credit on submission requires `Sanctioned` and sets `Disbursed`. A positive debit requires `Disbursed` and sets `Settled`. Merely requesting or saving a proposed journal has no such effect. The sanction reclassification converts a pledged amount to company currency using the invoice conversion rate and the row's outstanding precision. When an account currency differs from company currency, its account-currency amount uses the row amount. The disbursement builder sums nominal facility row amounts and uses account-currency fields. The single-currency examples below do not qualify a facility that mixes invoice currencies. ## Reconciliation example

With a 500.00 invoice, 500.00 pledged, and 20.00 lender fee, sanction moves 500.00 from trade to sanctioned receivables. Funding produces bank debit 480.00, fee debit 20.00, loan credit 500.00, discounted receivables debit 500.00 and sanctioned receivables credit 500.00. A customer collection of 300.00 then debits bank and credits discounted receivables. The invoice still has 200.00 outstanding. Repaying the facility before maturity debits the loan and credits bank by 500.00 and moves 200.00 to unpaid discounted receivables. The business retains a claim of 200.00, has incurred expense of 20.00, and owes the lender zero. The net change in bank is +280.00 before eventual collection of the remaining 200.00. See `FIN-001`–`FIN-004` in the [vectors](financial-examples.json).

Two boundaries deserve an explicit implementation decision. On the maturity date the settlement helper omits the unpaid reclassification, while subsequent collection routing still selects the unpaid account in `Settled`. Also, on early settlement the helper reclassifies the full live invoice outstanding even when the pledged row was smaller. Preserve these behaviors in a compatibility profile and compare control-account balances before changing either policy.
