# Six reports after settlement

Use one company and reporting currency with opening bank and equity of 1,000.00. The supplier receipt, bill and remittance, physical transformation, customer shipment, invoice, return and cash settlement all belong to the same reporting period. Read posted records after settlement. For the open-item reports select the corresponding party, the reporting date and ageing by due date. Include both raw and finished-goods warehouses in the stock balance.

For each account, signed closing balance equals opening debit less opening credit plus period debit less period credit. Show the magnitude on its resulting side. Open-item clearing can leave a zero account balance after substantial period activity. A turnover report retains both sides of that activity.

## Financial statements and open items

| Report | Calculation and expected result |
|---|---|
| Balance sheet | Bank 940.20 + recoverable tax 34.80 + inventory 134.80 = assets 1,109.80. Liabilities 27.00 + contributed capital 1,000.00 + provisional period profit 82.80 = 1,109.80. |
| Profit and loss | Net posted sales are 240.00 − 60.00 = 180.00. Net inventory expense is 129.60 − 32.40 = 97.20. Profit is 82.80. |
| Accounts receivable | The customer's posted receipts and allocations settle the receivable. The open-item result contains zero rows at the cut-off. The account's period turnover is still 276.00 on each side. |
| Accounts payable | The supplier's 266.80 bill is fully remitted and allocated. The supplier's open-item result contains zero rows at the cut-off. |

## Stock balance

| Stock and warehouse | Opening quantity / value | Inward quantity / value | Outward quantity / value | Closing quantity / value | Closing unit value |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Raw material, input warehouse | 0 / 0.00 | 20 / 232.00 | 15 / 162.00 | 5 / 70.00 | 14.00 |
| Finished product, dispatch warehouse | 0 / 0.00 | 6 / 194.40 | 4 / 129.60 | 2 / 64.80 | 32.40 |

The finished quantity received includes one physically returned unit. Closing inventory value of 134.80 reconciles to the two inventory accounts. A commercial credit without a physical return would require different stock movement and cost-of-sales rows. The figures use the report's selected company currency and quantity basis. Repeating the example under a different presentation language preserves all six results.
