## Published Video Interactions

Script Report. The reference record is Video. Declared inputs include `from_date`, `to_date`.

Named output fields constructed in this report include `publish_date`, `title`, `duration`, `view_count`, `like_count`, `dislike_count`, `comment_count`. The structured entry gives their enclosing conditions and builders.
