# Payroll loan recovery and payment gateway settlement

A salary slip, a loan repayment, a payment request, a provider capture and a bank statement each have their own identity. Payroll values a loan at the end of its pay period. Lending allocates the collection against demands. A gateway requests or receives funds. The accounting voucher records the local financial effect. Read the [interface catalog](../../schemas/data/external-record-contracts.json) for the exchanged fields.

## Salary deduction and loan repayment

| Stage | Record and decision |
|---|---|
| Select | With lending installed, select submitted loans whose company and applicant match the salary slip, salary recovery flag is set and status differs from Closed. The lookup uses the applicant identifier. An empty slip loan table is filled from loans with a nonzero amount due. Existing rows are retained and rechecked. With lending absent the guarded methods return without calling a provider. |
| Prepare the period | For eligible term loans, the demand-based provider prepares accruals and daily demands through salary end. Payroll also contains a conditional call for a provider without a demand record. That route requires a different accrual entry point, which the inspected demand-based provider does not supply. |
| Price the row | The provider checks loan read permission. Its due demands are submitted, have a demand date on or before the valuation date and have a positive outstanding balance at currency precision. Payroll copies payable total, payable principal and interest. These fields can have different totals because penalties or charges may also be due. When a payroll user edits a row, the caller sums its principal, interest and total separately and rejects a row total above the newly quoted payable amount. |
| Submit | A nonzero row on a slip with nonnegative net pay requests a normal repayment. The constructor inserts a draft, the caller saves and submits it, then stores the submitted repayment ID on the salary row. Product identity is fetched through the linked loan. The provider record defaults the repayment kind to Normal Repayment. The constructor's payment-type argument and the provider's declared repayment-type field have different names. Other repayment types cannot be inferred from this payroll call. |
| Allocate | During ordinary validation the provider queries amounts for the value date with update locking, refreshes the payable components and allocates the amount paid. The selected collection sequence comes from the loan product, then the company for the corresponding loan classification. A missing required sequence rejects allocation. The payroll component amounts are inputs to the handoff. The repayment allocations and linked demands establish what was actually collected. |
| Post and cancel | When company loan accounting is enabled, normal principal collections credit the loan account and normal interest collections credit the product’s interest receivable. Salary recovery debits payroll payable. Keep a separate mapping for penalties and charges. With loan accounting disabled the repayment produces no lending general-ledger map. Cancellation of the slip invokes cancellation on each linked repayment ID. The provider reverses the affected demand and paid balances and applies the relevant accounting reversal. |

**Dates and accounts.** Payroll supplies the slip’s posting date and explicitly supplies salary end as the value date. The provider resets posting date to the current processing date/time during ordinary repayment validation. Its ordinary ledger rows take the date of that posting timestamp. A repost uses its own processing-date branch. Demand allocation continues to use the value date. Store the salary posting date, repayment posting timestamp and effective value date separately. The batch payable account applies whenever the slip names a payroll batch. Only a slip without a batch uses the company default payroll payable. An empty account on a named batch does not trigger that fallback.

The provider recognizes the salary path through the workforce fields on Loan Repayment. When the salary flag is set, a payroll payable account is required. Supplying the account sets the salary flag if necessary. For a normal nonsalary repayment the payment account can be supplied explicitly, resolved from the mode-of-payment account for the company, resolved from the bank account, or obtained from the loan product, in that order. A mandatory posting account missing at journal composition blocks that posting. Use the payroll party-accounting setting when interpreting employee detail on the accounting rows.

**Worked recovery.** Payroll values a 200 principal and 15 interest obligation on 31 January. The slip has posting date 2 February. Its repayment is validated and submitted on 4 February. With loan accounting enabled, an allocation of 200 principal and 15 interest debits payroll payable 215, credits loan principal 200 and credits interest receivable 15, dated 4 February and valued at 31 January. Deduct 215 once from the cash salary. A row of 216 fails the caller’s payable cap. Cancelling the slip targets its saved repayment ID. If completion is uncertain before that ID is recorded, reconcile the loan, value date, amount, payroll payable and effective repayment before creating another. The inspected constructor has no salary-row idempotency argument.

## Exit settlement requires a different adapter

The workforce exit caller processes receivables whose component is Loan. For each loan it reads a submitted repayment schedule and calculates amounts as of the settlement transaction date. Its intended processing contract differs from the provider declarations at several decision points.

| Caller assumption | Provider observation and consequence |
|---|---|
| Check each schedule row’s accrued flag, create an accrual using one dictionary and mark that row accrued. | The current schedule declares a demand-generation flag. The current accrual constructor expects separate loan, base, interest, process, period/date, interest-type and rate arguments. The caller’s single-dictionary invocation cannot construct that accrual with this signature. |
| Use each receivable’s balance when creating its repayment. | The caller first collects the loan IDs and then loops over those IDs. The amount passed for each loan is taken from the final receivable in the original scan, even if that last receivable was a different component. |
| Settlement date identifies both the repayment and its obligations. | The exit caller passes settlement transaction date as the posting argument and omits the separate value date. The provider defaults the value date to the current day and resets the posting timestamp during normal validation. |
| Reverse repayments and accruals through loan plus settlement date. | Cancellation looks up a submitted repayment by loan and posting date and selects submitted accruals by the same fields. It has no origin ID to distinguish unrelated same-day transactions. The date filter can also miss a repayment processed on another day. |

An independent exit settlement should bind each receivable to its own loan and amount, pass the settlement value date explicitly, identify the actual due demands and retain each created repayment and accrual reference for reversal. For receivables A = 600 and B = 250, map 600 to A and 250 to B. A later unrelated receipt for B on the same date remains effective when the exit is reversed. These are acceptance conditions for the independent adapter. The supplied caller/provider combination does not establish successful execution for this route.

## Payment request, gateway and financial outcome

| Event | Input and business effect |
|---|---|
| Configure the route | Gateway identity, settings record type and optional controller reference identify the provider. If the controller reference is null the resolver loads singleton settings named for that gateway. Otherwise it loads the named settings record. A missing settings record causes a resolution error. A company gateway account binds gateway, company, payment account, currency and Email or Phone channel. Currency comes from the ledger account. Setting the default clears other defaults for that company. If no other default exists it becomes default. The request lookup accepts an explicit gateway-account selection or the company default. |
| Submit the request | Set outstanding in party-account currency. Outward requests enter Initiated. Inward requests enter Requested. An inward phone request validates currency and calls the configured controller’s phone operation. The phone amount is the grand total when there are no completed integration requests. Otherwise the caller sums their stored request amounts. A nonphone inward request attempts to set a URL and, unless muted, follows the mail path. URL setup requires a payment account, gateway and a successful optional submission check. An error inside that check suppresses URL generation. |
| Build checkout | The controller validates transaction currency and, when supplied, its minimum-amount check. The checkout contract receives the rounded request grand total in request currency, company/title, subject, payer name/email, payment-request identity, currency and gateway. The request references the original invoice/order independently of the provider session. |
| Handle provider completion | The core authorization callback accepts Authorized or Completed when the request is not already Paid. On Phone it sets Paid and zero outstanding without creating a Payment Entry in this callback. Other channels build and submit an accounting payment against the reference, and optionally generate the sales invoice. Verify provider success, local accounting and bank settlement individually. |
| Reconcile currency | The payment builder uses the request outstanding in party-account currency. Where party-account currency equals company currency and differs from the request currency, it divides outstanding by the reference document’s conversion rate and rounds to request grand-total precision. An outstanding 360 at 18 company units per foreign unit yields 20 request units in this branch. Preserve each payment-term allocation’s request identity. |
| Handle failure or cancellation | A provider redirect to checkout or a success page is insufficient to confirm a local receipt. In the inspected card connector, capture can mark the integration request Completed before the callback. A callback exception is logged. An uncaptured charge or a charge exception can follow the failure redirect without an explicit Failed write on that direct-charge path. Local cancellation marks the request Cancelled after its guard. For a Paid request the guard checks for a noncancelled payment reference with the same reference name. Cancellation alone does not establish a provider refund. |

For example, provider capture C1 succeeds for the 20-unit request but the callback fails to submit a local voucher. Reconcile the captured amount, currency, merchant, provider reference and original request first. If no effective voucher exists, complete the missing local allocation under the installation’s recovery procedure. A second provider charge would overcollect. A separate refund needs its own provider result and corresponding local reversal. The phone branch and the timing of the card callback are observed code paths. The reconciliation sequence is an independent operating requirement.
