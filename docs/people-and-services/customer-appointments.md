# Customer appointments, service commitments and service billing

## Reserve an appointment

The public booking command takes a local date, time, a valid named guest time zone and contact details. Convert to the site's time before comparing schedules. The portal requires booking to be enabled and applies a request rate limit. The created appointment starts Unverified. A fresh submission can be refused when the slot is already full, even though the unverified document itself occupies no capacity. A manual appointment must reference a customer or lead if a party kind is supplied and cannot use the unverified state.

Reject a past start. With scheduling enabled, reject a date beyond the configured nonzero advance-day limit, a holiday or missing holiday list, or a slot whose full duration fails to fit an available weekday window. On the portal the start must also align to an integer multiple of duration from at least one containing window's start. Manual entries can use an intermediate start if still within a window. For positive capacity, count other open appointments with intersecting half-open intervals at the configured duration. Identical end/start endpoints touch without overlapping. Capacity zero bypasses this count. Changes to time and transitions to Open repeat the capacity check. Serialize competing checks, including when the slot currently has zero Open rows. Reserve the capacity decision before updating individual appointments.

1. Send the verification link to the supplied email and store its digest. Verification resolves the stored digest. Closed returns a settled closed result; Open is an idempotent already-verified success. For unsettled rows check the creation-based expiry and the scheduled time before changing state. A concurrent loss of capacity yields a validation result rather than a second open booking.
2. When verified, set Open, materialize the party/calendar/assignment and send confirmation. Prefer an existing customer found from email, then a lead; include contact email links. With no match create a lead. Prefer the latest lead opportunity's assignee if free; otherwise choose an available configured agent by daily workload. Share the appointment where necessary to support the assignment.
3. On reschedule synchronize the event and reassign an already busy agent if a free alternative exists. Closing/reopening synchronizes the event and Open/Closed work assignments; already cancelled assignments retain their state. Deleting the appointment deletes its linked event. Expired unverified records are swept according to the configured close/delete policy when expiry is nonzero.

With one agent and one 10:00–10:30 opening, two pending verifications may exist. Confirming the first makes the second fail admission. At 10:30 a new appointment can start. These service events carry no invoice or stock obligation.

## Choose a service-level agreement

An enabled tracking policy, document kind and priority (if set) constrain candidates. Candidate validity includes both beginning and ending dates against today's date. For nondefault agreements, apply the party scope and document condition. Rank the entity keys in the sequence customer, customer group and ancestors, territory and ancestors, then agreements lacking a matching entity. Append eligible defaults separately. Keep the document's already selected agreement if it is anywhere in this eligible list, including a lower-ranked default; otherwise take the first. Do not infer deterministic tie resolution across indistinguishable records without an order. For example, if the customer-specific agreement C and default D both qualify, a new document gets C, while a document already carrying D keeps D. This precedes the [service clock and hold accounting](service-and-support.md).

## Bind maintenance coverage to the delivered serial

The schedule validates the serial's item and customer and looks up its latest outward delivery on a submitted, noncancelled serial bundle on a delivery note or sales invoice. Missing bundle evidence leads to a legacy lookup. Existing warranty expiry or contract expiry **on or after** proposed maintenance start rejects that serial. A delivery on the proposed start day also fails the ordering check. Thus, with delivery April 1 and warranty through April 30, an April 30 contract start fails; May 1 can satisfy these date rules. On cancellation, for each serial in the cancelled schedule's bundle, recompute its expiry from the latest end of **other submitted** schedules for the same item covering that serial, or clear it. Remove schedule events. Avoid wiping still-valid earlier coverage by clearing all serial expiries blindly.

## Costed time, task access and invoicing

A submitted time row can change both its formerly linked task/project aggregates and its new ones. For an ordinary caller, require selection access to every affected task and project, including the current project obtained from each task. When editing already submitted work compare task, project, timestamps, actual hours, transaction/base cost and billing amounts, and completion marker. Include deleted rows and both images of modified rows. This prevents using permission to edit a timesheet to alter an inaccessible project's totals. A cancelled task rejects a new time log. In deriving a task status preserve Completed/Cancelled, otherwise no submitted logs gives Open, one or more gives Working unless any submitted time row is marked completed, in which case it gives Completed.

The invoice preparation command requires read access to the referenced timesheet. It rejects zero billable hours and exact exhaustion of billable hours. For an optional service item use remaining billable amount / remaining billable hours as rate and remaining billable hours as quantity. Eight billable hours worth 1,000 less three already billed at 300 produce 5 hours at **140**, not the original weighted-average rate of 125. Copy unbilled billable time-row identities to the invoice's time references. Use the timesheet's parent project first; absent this, set the invoice project only if the remaining billable row projects form a single distinct value. The operation returns an invoice draft. Invoicing and payment create their own monetary entries.  [Project costing](projects-and-time-billing.md)
