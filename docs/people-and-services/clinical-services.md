# Patient access, coverage and billing

## Reserve an appointment

Patient Appointment owns patient, practitioner, service unit, date, start time, duration and state. For a timed practitioner booking, compare same-date appointments of the same patient **or** practitioner. Closed and Cancelled appointments are excluded. Strict interior overlaps and equal starts conflict; one appointment ending exactly when the next starts is allowed. With overlapping appointments enabled on a service unit, overlapping candidates for that unit and other patients may be admitted up to its capacity (default one). Candidates with the same patient still conflict. This query starts from the patient/practitioner candidate set, so it cannot serve as an all-practitioner unit occupancy query. The separate check-in mode tests an exact patient/date/time/appointment-for and check-in flag. Preserve its different test.

Date-derived open, scheduled and no-show states coexist with manually retained checked-in, checked-out, closed, cancelled and no-show states. A change of date must respect the state guards. Insurance coverage creation and fee validity depend on the invoice popup, free-follow-up policy and practitioner configuration. Calendar events and service-request progress are separate linked records.

## Bill and reverse an appointment

The billing operation uses the patient's linked customer, appointment company and that company's receivable account. It creates and submits a sales invoice with a line referencing the appointment. On the payment popup path, a nonzero percentage reduces the original paid amount by that percentage. A nonzero absolute discount then replaces the computed paid amount with original paid less the absolute discount. Both invoice discount attributes may be set; this precedence describes the payment-row calculation. For a paid amount of 200, 10% and an absolute amount of 12, the payment row is 188. With only the percentage, it is 180. A mode and nonzero original paid amount create an immediate point-of-sale payment row. Otherwise the invoice can be unpaid. Persist the resulting invoice identity, paid amount and invoiced flag on the appointment.

On practitioner appointment cancellation, reverse linked insurance coverage, reactivate a linked service request and update fee validity. For an invoiced appointment the automated cancellation helper checks the popup setting and a single invoice item. It cancels a submitted matching single-line invoice. A multiple-line invoice requires review, protecting other appointment charges. A linked calendar event moves to Cancelled. Operational access, clinical record confidentiality and the payment/claims boundary need separate permission tests.

## Consume an outpatient follow-up entitlement

A Fee Validity record owns the start date, last eligible date, creation timestamp, visited count and allowed maximum. Past end date wins the status decision, including when the visit count also equals the limit. On or before the end date, an exact count match yields Completed, otherwise the status helper sets Active. An overshoot is a separate edge to qualify. The date coverage helper allows a visit on or after start **or** on or after the validity record's creation date. This allows a same-day consultation to use a future-start entitlement created that day. A visit predating both fails that particular test.

Only an appointment or encounter may be a visit. The lookup requires patient read access and, for an already-saved visit, read access to the visit itself. Route appointments by appointment date and encounters by encounter date. An explicit inpatient link or an admitted patient inpatient record sends the visit down the inpatient billing path. Use this boundary when testing an operator who can see a patient but cannot read the requested encounter.

## Encounter, service request and result

The encounter records patient, practitioner, visit time, appointment or inpatient stay, requested services and the identities of resulting orders. Submitting an encounter prepares a therapy plan when therapies were requested, creates the service and medication requests, attaches their identities to the originating rows, then marks the encounter complete. An unbooked encounter updates outpatient entitlement directly. An encounter linked to an appointment leaves that entitlement with the appointment, preventing two uses for a single visit.

Before cancelling, identify submitted service requests belonging to the encounter and cancel them. Check fee entitlement on an unbooked encounter. Reopen a linked appointment on cancellation, otherwise cancel the encounter's entitlement effect. An in-progress or completed therapy plan blocks cancellation. A cancellable plan retains its identity and moves to Cancelled. If a later check refuses the command, the earlier cancellations and appointment state must be reconciled as one transaction.

## Inpatient admission, occupancy and charges

The inpatient stay retains its originating encounter, scheduled admission, patient, discharge order, sequence of unit occupancies and billable items. Another stay for the patient in Admission Scheduled or Admitted blocks validation. Proposed discharge dates cannot precede scheduled admission. A completed occupancy cannot check out earlier than it checked in. A unit transfer recognizes a repeat assignment to the same still-held unit. For a new occupancy, the target must be vacant. Store its check-in and the procedural-transfer marker if applicable. Leaving a unit records checkout and frees that unit. Distinguish a temporary procedure location from a permanent change of accommodation.

For occupancy billing, group occupancy durations by billing item. Use the clock at evaluation for a still-open interval and its actual checkout for a closed interval. For each item compute the greater of (summed hours divided by hours per chargeable unit) and minimum billable quantity. The hours-per-unit default is one. Two occupancies of six hours each charged as the same item with eight hours per unit and minimum one yield 1.5 units. Price the item using the patient's billing customer, company and selling price list. A missing item mapping skips that group. A missing or zero rate warrants a warning. An unbilled line can be refreshed, while previously invoiced quantity must be distinguished before adding a further charge.

Discharge validates nursing tasks, the configured policy for unbilled services and incomplete service requests, checks out the occupancies, timestamps discharge and closes active nursing care plans for the admission. An active plan linked to the wrong patient loses the incorrect inpatient link upon closure. Retain the history of clinical acts and billable quantities independently of payment. An operator authorized to bill a bed or receive a payment needs only the permitted billing facts, while access to examination findings, orders and individual results follows the separate patient/encounter permissions.
