# People, customer relationships and service delivery

| Follow a business event | Where to find the rules |
|---|---|
| Approve headcount, advertise, interview, offer, evaluate and develop people | [Recruitment and performance](recruitment-and-performance.md) |
| Hire, transfer, promote, separate or settle an employee | [Workforce and employment](workforce-and-employment.md) |
| Schedule shifts, derive attendance, book time off or encash leave | [Attendance and leave](attendance-and-leave.md) |
| Evaluate compensation, benefits and tax, post salaries and settle withheld pay | [Payroll and benefits](payroll-and-benefits.md) and [payroll journals and disbursement](payroll-posting.md) |
| Fund and recover advances, sanction claims, pay referral bonuses | [Employee expenses and advances](employee-expenses-and-advances.md) and [recruiting commitments and employee money](employee-recovery.md) |
| Convert a prospect, manage a forecast, prepare a quotation or sync a service customer | [Customer relationships and sales](customer-relationships-and-sales.md) and [commercial handoff](commercial-handoff.md) |
| Book a customer, transfer a live call, measure first response and schedule maintenance | [Appointments and billing](customer-appointments.md), [call routing](incoming-calls.md) and [service and support](service-and-support.md) |
| Schedule task dependencies, record hours and invoice only the remaining value | [Projects and time billing](projects-and-time-billing.md) and [project costing](../transactions/project-costing.md) |
| Value collateral, accrue interest, allocate repayments, restructure or close a credit exposure | [Lending and employee recovery](lending.md) |
| Enrol a student, reserve teaching resources, assess marks and bill fees | [Education](education.md) |
| Reserve clinical capacity, manage stays and service entitlements, bill and reverse care | [Clinical services](clinical-services.md) |
| Collect gifts, renew memberships, review grants and manage participation | [Donations, membership and grants](nonprofit.md) |

The [logical model](../model/README.md) defines records and relationships. The [calculation catalogue](../calculations/README.md) provides worked input and outcome cases. Approval, attendance, entitlements, invoice posting and cash settlement each have their own decision and persistent identity. A submitted salary slip, a completed appointment or an approved grant provides a reference for the next authorized action.
