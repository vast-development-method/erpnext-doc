# Operations

The operation catalogs provide semantic names, accepted bindings and ordered decision structures for 33,360 inventoried operations. Use the operation index to find an exact contract, the decision semantics to interpret its nodes, and domain chapters to understand its business purpose. Supporting checks and excluded utilities remain explicitly classified.
